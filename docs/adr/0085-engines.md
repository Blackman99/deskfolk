# 引擎：应用自己的环节怎么跑，按对话选 / Engines: how the app's own steps run, chosen per conversation

Status: implemented 2026-10-11, at every engine level (a step a level does not run yet stays off whatever its engine says). Builds on ADR 0055 (reading), ADR 0064 (built-in prompts), ADR 0077 (built-in models), ADR 0082 and 0083 (Roles, call records). Not the same thing as the engine level of ADR 0040 (`engine_level`), which is how far a data folder has been migrated.

## 背景 / Context

你说轮次引擎应当是动态的、可以自己设计的，应用内置一套你在实践里磨出来的，Bot 也应当能改。之前的讨论把它分成两层：骨架——你的话按顺序进来、叫停就是停、先归属再叫醒、工作项、读出「停下 / 继续」之后怎么停怎么放——每一条都是一次事故换来的，留在代码里；挂在骨架上的部分——应用自己的哪些调用跑、各自的阈值——做成数据，可以按对话选、可以改。提示词和模型照旧是全局的（ADR 0064、0077），在分工页上和用到它们的环节画在一起。你又要了三件事：内置做视频、写代码两套和一套更通用更简化的默认；用已经有的对话记录来调；首次进入应用就能选引擎和 Bot 模板。

记录（截至 2026-10-11，642 轮、125 件事、约 $1,122 的花费）：视频占花费的 92%；MV 的第 09 张任务被你退回 8 次，一次也没挂在要求上，「连续 3 次」从没成立（ADR 0085 同期的修复已经让你的退回算数）；视频导演约的 22 次回看有 13 次醒来什么都没做、平均也跑了 53 跳；书记员在视频里 79 次调用记下 109 条要求，在通用对话里 30% 是空的，写代码时两次调用记下一条；模型升级只把 Claude Code 的思考档调高了，没有效果；完工复盘 15 次里 5 次改了记忆或技能，有两次复盘的是一段旧迁移把问答开出来的「事」；整理器 75 次静下来整理全选了「继续」，但它同时把看板和规划写对，这部分有用。写代码的数据很少。

You said the turn engine should be dynamic and designable, with a built-in one from your own practice, and that Bots should be able to change it too. The discussion before split it in two: the skeleton — your lines taken in order, a stop that is a stop, filing before waking, work items, what a reading of stop or go on does — each rule paid for by an incident, stays code; what hangs on it — which of the app's own calls run, and their thresholds — becomes data, chosen per conversation and editable. Prompts and models stay global (ADR 0064, 0077), drawn on Roles beside the steps that use them. You asked for three more things: two built-ins for video and for coding plus a more general, simpler default; tuning them on the conversations so far; and choosing an engine and Bot templates on first launch.

The record (to 2026-10-11: 642 turns, 125 jobs, about $1,122): video is 92% of the spend; the MV's ticket 09 was sent back eight times, never tied to a requirement, so "three in a row" never formed (fixed alongside this ADR: your send-backs count); 13 of the director's 22 check-backs woke to nothing new after some 53 hops each; the scribe wrote 109 entries from 79 calls in video, came back empty on 30% of general lines, and wrote one entry from two calls in coding; escalation only raised Claude Code's effort, to no effect; 5 of 15 retrospectives changed a memory or skill, and two looked back on "jobs" an old migration had made of questions; 75 of 75 settles chose "continue", though the same call keeps the board and plan true, which is worth having. There is little coding data.

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **引擎是挂在骨架上的环节 / An engine is the steps hung on the skeleton.** 一个引擎说十六个环节各自跑不跑、带什么数（`PLAYBOOK_STEPS`，协议里）：你的一句话（排队上限、读句、书记员）、群里没点名的一句（判断下场）、Bot 的一轮（跳数节奏、压缩上下文、收尾自检、整理、Bot 自己约的回看、监督器接手）、交上来（验收检查和看图判定、模型升级、能力天花板）、你接受交付（完工复盘）、你推翻放行（反思）、输入建议。能关的是书记员、收尾自检、整理、模型升级、完工复盘、反思；读句、判断下场、压缩、验收检查这些锁着——关了骨架就不成立（一份交付等你的检查跑，检查不跑就永远等着）——它们的数照样能改。数字都是整数、有范围，默认值就是原来代码里的常量。基准测试的开关（ablation）照旧有效，并且压过引擎。/ An engine says, for each of sixteen steps (`PLAYBOOK_STEPS`, in the protocol), whether it runs and with what numbers: your line (the intake ceiling, reading, the scribe), a group line naming nobody (judgement), a Bot's turn (hop pacing, compaction, the closing check, the settle, a Bot's own check-backs, the supervisor's pick-ups), a hand-in (acceptance checks and the picture judge, escalation, the capability ceiling), a delivery you accept (retrospective), an approval you overturn (reflection), and the composer. The scribe, the closing check, the settle, escalation, retrospective and reflection can be switched off; reading, judgement, compaction and the acceptance checks are locked — off, the skeleton would not hold (a hand-over waits for your checks to run, and checks that never run hold it for good) — but their numbers can change. Numbers are whole and ranged, and default to the constants the code had. The benchmark's switches still apply and win over an engine.

2. **内置三套，改动只存差别 / Three built in; what you change is kept as a difference.** 通用、视频、代码是代码里的三份差别（`BUILTIN_PLAYBOOKS`）。你改的只存你和内置不同的那几项，每改一次是一条只增不删的修改记录（谁改的、改之前和之后）；没改过的那项跟着内置的新默认走，所以不用像提示词那样三方合并，「和内置不同」的标记、恢复一步都直接从差别里来。也可以以一个引擎为底另建一个自己的，起名字。/ General, video and coding are three differences in code (`BUILTIN_PLAYBOOKS`). What you change keeps only what differs from the built-in, and every change is an append-only revision (who, before and after); a field you never touched follows a new default, so there is no three-way merge as prompts need, and "differs from built-in" and resetting a step come straight from the difference. You can also make your own engine from any of them and name it.

3. **三套各是什么 / What the three are.**
   - **通用（默认）/ General (the default).** 不要书记员、完工复盘和反思：不在你背后记要求、改记忆、出提议卡片。整理照旧，它让一件事的看板对得上。/ No scribe, no retrospective, no reflection: nothing written down, remembered or proposed behind your back. The settle stays: it keeps a job's board true.
   - **视频 / Video.** 书记员照旧；整理等静下来一分钟，一批镜头交上来只整理一次；Bot 自己约的回看最少隔 15 分钟、每小时最多 3 次；不升级模型；没过的交付到第 5 次就停下来问你（原来 6 次）。/ The scribe stays; the settle waits a minute so a fan-out of shots is filed once; a Bot's own check-backs are at least 15 minutes apart and at most three an hour; no escalation; the ceiling's backstop comes at the fifth failed hand-over (was six).
   - **代码 / Coding.** 不要书记员，其余照旧：数据太少，不编数字。/ No scribe; the rest as before: there is too little data to invent numbers.

4. **哪个引擎管这一次 / Which engine a call runs under.** 关于一件事的调用照那件事所在的会话；不关于事的照它所在的会话——Bot 之间的私聊和一件事的线程照它们从哪个会话来；那个会话指定了引擎就用它，没有就用设置里的默认（`default_playbook`），再没有就是通用。删掉的自定义引擎当成默认。每条调用记录记着当时用的引擎和它改过几次，流程图上点开一步看得到。/ A call about a job runs under the engine of the job's conversation; one about no job under its own conversation's — a Bot↔Bot direct or a job's thread under the one it came from; that conversation's engine if it names one, else Settings' default (`default_playbook`), else general. A custom engine since removed reads as the default. Every call record keeps the engine it ran under and how many times it had been changed, shown when a step on the flow is opened.

5. **在哪里选 / Where it is chosen.** 分工页按流程图的样子画一个引擎，点开一步就地开关、改数，改的是那个引擎；页头选看哪个、设为默认、另建。群的设置和私聊里 Bot 的「操作」里选这个会话用哪个。首次进入应用先选引擎、再选 Bot 模板，建的 Bot 的私聊用这个引擎，选了两个以上还建一个群、负责人是模板里的那位；全局默认仍是通用。新建 Bot 也能从模板开始。/ Roles draws an engine the way the flow is drawn; a step opens in place to switch it or change its numbers, in that engine; its header picks the engine shown, sets the default and makes a new one. A group's settings and a Bot's Actions in your direct choose the conversation's engine. First launch asks for an engine, then Bot templates: the Bots' directs run that engine, two or more also get a group with the template's lead; the global default stays general. A new Bot can start from a template too.

## 后果 / Consequences

- **升级后没指定引擎的对话都是通用 / On upgrade every conversation that names none runs general.** 也就是书记员、完工复盘、反思在那些对话里不跑了。视频导演的私聊合入时由你确认后设成视频。/ So the scribe, retrospectives and reflections stop there. The director's direct is set to video on landing, once you confirm.
- **骨架的三处修复和这个 ADR 同期 / Three fixes to the skeleton came with it.** 你退回也算进能力天花板；任务在等你时 Bot 约不上回看；一段每次启动都跑的旧迁移不再给当场回答的轮补开一件事（之前开出来的 34 件先不动）。它们对所有引擎都一样。/ Your send-backs count towards the ceiling; a Bot cannot book a check-back on a ticket that waits on you; an old migration that ran on every start no longer gives a turn that answered in place a job of its own (the 34 it made are left). They are the same under every engine.
- **没做的 / Not done.** 看图判定放过了 10 次 MV 交付、其中 7 次被你以人物不像退回：那是 `call.standard` 提示词的事，要按引擎分提示词，留给下一步；现在可以在分工页上直接改这段提示词。休眠的阈值和看图的每日预算是全局的扫一遍或总预算，不按对话。修改记录还不能撤销。自定义环节（只能从一张固定的效果清单里选）、Bot 提议改引擎（走批准卡片）、拿过去的话回放评估，是之后的几步；数据结构已经留了位置（差别带版本号，环节 id 是字符串，修改记录带 Bot、轮次、批准和消息）。/ The picture judge passed 10 MV hand-overs, 7 of which you sent back on likeness: that is the `call.standard` prompt, and per-engine prompts are a later step; the prompt can be edited on Roles now. Dormancy and the vision daily budget are global sweeps or budgets, not per conversation. Revisions cannot be undone yet. Custom steps (effects from a fixed list only), Bots proposing engine changes (through an approval card) and replaying past lines to evaluate a change come later; the data leaves room (a versioned difference, step ids as strings, revisions carrying the Bot, turn, approval and message).
- **基准可以比较引擎 / The benchmark can compare engines.** 黄金路径评测多了 `--playbook`，结果按引擎分组。/ The golden-path eval takes `--playbook`, and its results group by engine.

## 补记 / Addendum (2026-10-12)

**视频引擎 200 跳收工具 / The video engine takes the tools at 200 hops.** 同一天 Claude Agent 的一段改成跟着引擎的跳数上限走（[ADR 0061](0061-claude-agent-runner.md) 的补记），之前它只有固定的 200 步。视频导演跑在 Claude Agent 上，最近几段长活走到 190、200、200、200 步，都在干活；照默认的 160，它每段会早 40 步收尾、多交接四分之一。视频引擎因此把 `pacing.hop_limit` 设成 200，节奏和原来一样，变的只是到顶时的收尾：先交代做到哪，而不是记成卡住。其余引擎仍是 160。 / The same day a Claude Agent segment began to follow its engine's hop limit ([ADR 0061](0061-claude-agent-runner.md)'s addendum); before, it had only a fixed 200. 视频导演 runs on Claude Agent, and its recent long segments ran 190, 200, 200 and 200 steps, all of them working; at the default 160 each would close 40 steps sooner, a quarter more hand-overs. So the video engine sets `pacing.hop_limit` to 200: the same cadence as before, changing only how a segment ends at the top — saying where it stands instead of failing as stuck. The other engines stay at 160.
