# 完工复盘：你接受的一件事交付后，做过它的 Bot 回看一次，改进自己的记忆和技能 / The retrospective after a delivery

Status: implemented at engine level 8 (`ENGINE_LEVELS.learning`, the default), beside [ADR 0050](0050-quality-events-and-lessons.md) and [ADR 0051](0051-narrowed-reflection.md). It amends ADR 0050 §7 (below).

你要的是：一件事落地、你认可之后，Bot 自己回顾这件事踩了哪些坑、哪些做法让你一再反馈返工、哪些下次做类似的事还该照做，记下来，并且对照已有的记忆和技能修正、提炼，而不是越堆越多。8 级之前的学习跳做不到这件事：它在一条链安静 15 分钟后就跑，按「是不是模型的问题」开口，一次只看一条链；ADR 0050 §7 把它停掉之后，8 级起什么都不往记忆里写。实际的返工却留在了别处：2026-10-06 的《全职猎人》短片被退回三次（「动画不是真人」「配音用日文，而且要带感情」「奇犽的妹妹是男声……没有放电的音效」）、放行后又投诉一次，这些话进了需求台账，只在那个会话里继承；视频导演的记忆和技能「关键帧出片」一个字没变。

You asked for this: once a job lands and you accept it, the Bot looks back on where it tripped up, which of its ways made you keep giving feedback and sending work back, and what to do again on similar work — writes that down, and corrects and sharpens what it already remembers and knows rather than piling more on. The learning hop before level 8 could not: it ran after a chain had been quiet for 15 minutes, opened with "was it the model's fault", and saw one chain at a time; since ADR 0050 §7 stopped it, nothing at level 8 writes a memory after the fact. The rework stayed elsewhere: the 2026-10-06 short film was sent back three times ("animation, not live action", "dub it in Japanese, with feeling", "Killua's sister has a man's voice… no sound for the lightning") and complained about once after an approval; those words went into the requirements ledger and are inherited in that one conversation; the director's memories and its skill 关键帧出片 did not change by a word.

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **何时，由应用定 / When, decided by the app.** 调度器每一拍取最早一件已交付（`tasks.stage = delivered`，ADR 0046 的放行一路走完）、交付后安静满 30 分钟、7 天内的规划。30 分钟是因为放行之后紧跟一句投诉很常见（《全职猎人》放行 1 分钟后就被投诉返工）：投诉重开规划，复盘等下一次交付。日程的规划不复盘（它本来就是无人值守、由门禁放行的）。一件事里交过东西的 Bot 各复盘一次（交得最多的前三个）；Bot 已删除的跳过；有叫停罩着这个 Bot 或这件事时等着，解除后再跑。重开后又交付，是新的一次复盘，只看上次复盘之后又出的问题。每天两美元、12 次封顶（花费用途 `retrospect`，记在 `organize` 下）。先记一行 `pending` 再调用，重启后还在 pending 的十分钟后记为失败（interrupted），不重试。 / On each scheduler tick the oldest plan that was delivered (`tasks.stage = delivered`, ADR 0046's approvals all the way through), has stayed so for 30 minutes and is within 7 days is taken. Thirty minutes because a complaint right after an approval is common (the short film was sent back one minute after its approval): a complaint reopens the plan, and the retrospective waits for the next delivery. A routine's plan is not looked back on (it runs unattended and its gates approve it). Each Bot that handed something over in the job looks back once (the three that handed over most); a deleted Bot is skipped; under a stop over the Bot or the plan it waits for the lift. Delivered again after a reopen, it is another retrospective, over only what went wrong since the last one. At most $2 and 12 a day (spend purpose `retrospect`, billed as `organize`). A `pending` row is written before the call; one a restart left pending is failed (interrupted) after ten minutes, not retried.
2. **没出问题就不调 / Nothing went wrong, no call.** 只有这个 Bot 在这件事里出过问题才调用：它的交付被你退回、被审查打回或检查没过，投诉返工，你在交付之后才第一次说的要求，渲染丢失或失败，搜索超时，教训复犯，或者一张任务它交了不止一次（质量事件的 `execution`、`unclear`、`pipeline`、`review_miss` 类；模型的失败形状和应用自己的停滞不算）。一次就过、什么都没出的，记一行 `skipped: nothing_to_learn`，不花钱——学不到东西的复盘只会写出空话。 / A call is made only when something went wrong for this Bot in the job: a hand-over of its sent back by you, rejected by a review or failed by its checks, a complaint's rework, a requirement you first said after a hand-over, a render lost or failed, a search timed out, a lesson recurring, or a ticket it had to hand over more than once (quality events of the `execution`, `unclear`, `pipeline` and `review_miss` kinds; a model's failure shape and the app's own stalls do not count). Through first time with nothing wrong, it is recorded as `skipped: nothing_to_learn` at no cost — a retrospective with nothing to learn from writes filler.
3. **读什么 / What it reads.** 当事的 Bot 用它自己的模型（你钉的、它的默认、端点默认——7 级没有轮次的那次决定；Claude Agent 运行方式的 Bot 也一样走端点，见 ADR 0061：应用自己的判断用端点），读这件事的记录，不读转录：你在这件事里的原话（最早 8 句加最新 32 句）；每一次交付和它的结局——你退回时说的话、审查结论、没过的检查、它自己交付时写的说明；放行后的投诉；这件事的需求（说过几次、是不是交付之后才说的）；它的质量事件计数、反复失败的工具调用、被提档和收尾被退回的次数；它的全部记忆（含你停用的，标明别碰）、它自己的技能（正文读到 16000 字为止，读不下的只给名字）、项目技能和采用过的清单项；它最近三次复盘写了什么、哪些被你撤销了（这件事上一次交付的那次，或一天以前的；刚写下、还没经过任何检验的结论不拿来下判断）。 / The Bot involved, on its own model (its pin, its default, the endpoint's default — the level-7 decision with no turn; a Bot run as a Claude Agent goes through the endpoint too, ADR 0061: the app's own judgements use endpoints), reads the job's record, not the transcript: your words on the job (the first 8 and the latest 32); every hand-over and what became of it — what you said sending it back, review verdicts, checks that failed, the note it wrote handing over; complaints after an approval; the job's requirements (how often you said each, and whether only after a hand-over); its quality events by kind, tool calls that kept failing, how often it was stepped up and its ending sent back; all its memories (with the ones you turned off, marked hands-off), its own skills (bodies read up to 16,000 characters; past that, the name only), project skills and adopted checklist items; what its last three retrospectives wrote and which of those changes you undid (this job's previous delivery, or ones at least a day old: a conclusion just written, not yet tested by anything, is not judged).
4. **答什么 / What it answers.** 一个 JSON 对象：一句话总结；踩的坑、让你返工的做法、下次照做的，各至多 5 条；对以前结论的判断（这件事真检验过的：仍然成立、又犯了、已不成立）；以及几处改动。规则写在提示词里：找的是根因（它的哪个做法、默认或没核对的地方），不是复述你的话；只记跨出这件事仍然成立的；台账里这件事的要求不抄成记忆，要记的是怎么做才不会被同样地退回、被你纠正过的默认值、跨事成立的偏好；一条规矩只放一个地方：工序的一步进技能，偏好、默认和事实进记忆；只改这件事能作证的；没东西可学就什么都不改。 / One JSON object: a one-sentence summary; where it tripped up, what made you send work back, what to do again, at most five each; a verdict on earlier conclusions this job put to the test (still holds, happened again, no longer holds); and a few changes. The prompt sets the rules: a root cause (which of its choices, defaults or unchecked assumptions), not your words restated; only what holds beyond this job; not this job's requirements copied from the ledger, but how to work so it is not sent back the same way again, defaults you had to correct, preferences that hold across jobs; one rule in one place: a step of a procedure in the skill, preferences, defaults and facts in a memory; only what this job is evidence for; nothing to learn, nothing changed.
5. **应用来写，逐条核对 / The app writes, change by change.** 改动只有四种：记一条（同一主题就是覆盖；`replaces` 列出被并掉的旧记忆）、删一条、改自己的一条技能、新建一条技能。每处改动在自己的保存点里写，一处被拒不连累别的；每处都存下它替换掉的原文。上限写在代码里：记最多 3 条、删最多 3 条、技能最多 2 处（新建最多 1 条、正文不超过 6000 字）；记忆照工具的规矩（主题 40 字、正文 160 字、满 20 条时要先删或并掉一条才能新记）；你停用的记忆和技能不改；项目技能和别的 Bot 的技能不改；和项目技能同名的不新建。回答写到一半被截断、或不是一个完整的 JSON 对象，一处都不写，记为失败。 / Four kinds of change only: remember one (the same subject overwrites; `replaces` names the memories merged into it), forget one, edit one of its own skills, create one. Each is written in its own savepoint, so one refused takes no other with it, and each keeps what it replaced. The caps are in code: at most 3 remembered and 3 forgotten, at most 2 skill changes (1 created, a body of at most 6,000 characters); a memory follows the tool's rules (40-character subject, 160-character body; with 20 enabled, a new one needs one forgotten or merged away first); a memory or skill you turned off is not touched; a project skill or another Bot's skill is not changed; no new skill takes a project skill's name. An answer cut off mid-way, or not one whole JSON object, writes nothing and is recorded as failed.
6. **技能只能补，不能整段改写 / A skill is added to, never rewritten.** 改技能只有三种写法：在正文某段原文之后补一句（`after` + `add`，原文必须恰好出现一次）、在末尾追加一段（只给 `add`）、改正一句本身错了的话（`old` + `new`，一次只改一句，含有句号以外的句末标点或换行的不算一句）。另外，改完的正文里，原来反引号里的名字、文件名、链接、环境变量一个都不能少；少了，或者替换不止一句，这处改动整条不写，板上写明为什么。第一次在真实数据上跑时，模型把「4. **配音。**」整步改写成更好的规矩，同时丢掉了「一句台词一个文件」和 `voice`/`shot`/`offset`——技能没有历史版本，这样的丢失没人发现得了。读不下正文的长技能只能在末尾追加。 / A skill changes in three ways only: a sentence added right after a passage (`after` + `add`; the passage must appear exactly once), a passage appended at the end (`add` alone), or one sentence that is itself wrong corrected (`old` + `new`, one sentence at a time; a sentence-ending mark other than its last, or a line break, makes it more than one). And every name in backticks, file name, link and environment variable the skill had must still be in it afterwards. Missing one, or replacing more than a sentence, the change is not written, and the board says why. On the first run over real data the model rewrote step 4 into a better rule and dropped 「一句台词一个文件」 and `voice`/`shot`/`offset` with it — skills keep no history, so nobody could have noticed. A skill too long to read can only be appended to.
7. **不问你，能撤销 / Nothing asked, everything undoable.** 复盘不发消息、不出卡片、不发通知（[ADR 0058](0058-the-app-asks-only-when-it-needs-you.md)：这既不是放行，也不是只有你给得了的东西）。它出现在这件事的流程图侧栏「完工复盘」里：谁复盘的、总结、三张清单、对以前结论的判断，以及每一处改动——新记、改写、删掉、改了技能、新建技能，可以展开看原来和现在（技能只列改了的行），每处一个「撤销」；没写进去的写明为什么。撤销把原文放回去，但只在它还是复盘写下的样子时才做：之后你或 Bot 在一轮里又改过的，就是新的了，撤销会被拒（409）。复盘写的记忆在 Bot 资料页的记忆卡上标「完工复盘《…》写的」；Bot 在一轮里重写、或你改了它的文字，这个标就去掉。手机上也能撤销。 / A retrospective posts nothing, shows no card and sends no notification (ADR 0058: it lets nothing through and needs nothing only you can give). It shows on the plan's board, in the side panel's Retrospective block: who looked back, the summary, the three lists, the verdicts on earlier conclusions, and each change — remembered, rewritten, forgotten, a skill changed or made — opened to show before and now (for a skill, only the lines that changed), each with an Undo; one not made says why. Undo puts back what was replaced, but only while it is still as the retrospective wrote it: changed since by you or by the Bot in a turn, it is new, and the undo is refused (409). A memory a retrospective wrote is marked 「完工复盘《…》写的」 on the memory card in the Bot's profile; rewritten by the Bot in a turn, or its text changed by you, the mark goes. It can be undone from the phone too.
8. **它修订 ADR 0050 §7 / It amends ADR 0050 §7.** 0050 停掉的照样停着：没有链的复盘，不问模型「是不是模型不行」，你的回复不算选路反馈，质量事件本身不写进记忆，搜索超时的教训仍由应用执行。新加的只有这一件：一件你接受了的事交付之后，做过它的 Bot 检查自己的做法，把结论写进自己的记忆和技能——它读质量事件，但写下的是做法，不是事件；它不评价模型，也不提换模型。记忆仍然是 Bot 自己的（ADR 0021）：不跨 Bot 共享，你能看、改、停用、删。 / What 0050 stopped stays stopped: no chain is reviewed, no model is asked whether the model was at fault, your replies are not feedback on the model choice, quality events themselves are not written into memories, and a search's timeout is still a lesson the app enforces. Only this is added: after a job you accepted is delivered, the Bots that made it check their own conduct and write the conclusion into their own memories and skills — reading quality events, but writing ways of working, not events; judging no model, proposing no model switch. A memory is still the Bot's own (ADR 0021): not shared across Bots, and yours to read, change, turn off and delete.

## 缺口 / Not done

- **审查者不复盘**：只有交过东西的 Bot 复盘；审查者的误放行仍由 [ADR 0051](0051-narrowed-reflection.md) 的收窄的反思处理。 / A reviewer does not look back: only Bots that handed something over do; a reviewer's overturned approval is still ADR 0051's narrowed reflection.
- **没有交付阶段的规划**（5 级以下、一句话就答完的事）不复盘：没有放行，就没有「你认可了」这一刻。 / A plan with no hand-over stage (below level 5, a question answered in a line) is not looked back on: with no approval there is no moment you accepted it.
- **复盘不跨 Bot**：一个 Bot 学到的，别的 Bot 读不到；要共享，把技能共享成项目技能（ADR 0052）。 / What one Bot learns stays with it; to share it, share the skill as a project skill (ADR 0052).

## 取舍 / Trade-offs

- **记忆和技能偶尔说同一件事。** 提示词要求一条规矩只放一个地方，真实模型上大约一半的回答还是两边各写一遍；没有做去重——那要再一次模型调用，而重复的代价只是几十个字。 / Memory and skill sometimes say the same thing: the prompt asks for one place per rule, yet on the real model about half the answers wrote it into both. No deduplication is done — it would take another model call, and the cost of the duplicate is a few dozen characters.
- **技能改动常被拒。** 模型爱整步改写；宁可这处改动不写（板上写明原因、记忆照写），也不让技能悄悄丢东西。 / Skill edits are often refused: the model likes to rewrite whole steps; better that change not made (the board says why, and the memories are still written) than a skill that quietly loses something.
- **30 分钟等待。** 放行后的第一个半小时里投诉最常见；代价是结论晚半小时才进记忆。 / Thirty minutes' wait: complaints are commonest in the first half hour after an approval; the cost is that the conclusion reaches the memories half an hour later.
- **用 Bot 自己的模型。** 它最清楚自己怎么做的；判断错了，也只是一处你能撤销的改动。 / The Bot's own model: it knows best how it worked; a wrong judgement is still only a change you can undo.
