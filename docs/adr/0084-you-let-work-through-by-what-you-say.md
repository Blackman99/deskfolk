# 放行也是你的一句话 / You let work through by what you say

Status: implemented 2026-10-11, from engine level 5 (submissions). Takes back the last sentence of [ADR 0070](0070-the-app-stays-out-of-the-conversation.md) #5 (「放行仍然只在卡片上按：打字永远不算放行」), the approve/reject card of [ADR 0046](0046-submissions-and-reviews.md) and [ADR 0058](0058-the-app-asks-only-when-it-needs-you.md) §13, and with it ADR 0058 §15–18 (when that card goes up, comes down and goes up again); [ADR 0060](0060-large-jobs-sample-first.md)'s sample and last part are still yours to let through, by your word now.

ADR 0070 之后，退回已经是你的一句话：读句读出你在挑毛病，就直接转回返工。放行却还是一张卡片：一份交付没有审查者、也没有你确认过的检查撑着（还有一段话、整理跳的读法、同模型的审查、大活的样片和最后一件），应用就贴一张「看过之后，放行或者退回」的卡，等你按。ADR 0081 刚把叫停改成「你再说话就接着」，你说：「关于放行行为，不应当由用户进行决策，就跟停止行为一样，通过用户的消息来判断这个任务是被返工了，还是通过了。」库里的记录也是这样：按过的放行卡之后，你下一句话多半是在说下一件事（「电脑磁盘的系统数据怎么特别大」「这个端点的 qwen-audio-3.0-tts-plus 支持哪些语音」），放行是顺手按掉的；要改的时候，你本来就是用话说的。

After ADR 0070 a send-back was already a line of yours: read as finding fault, the work went back to rework. Letting work through was still a card: a hand-over with no reviewer and no check you confirmed behind it (and words handed over, the organizer's reading, a review on the producer's own model, a large job's sample and last part) put up 「看过之后，放行或者退回」 and waited for your press. With stops just made "your next word goes on" (ADR 0081), you said: "Letting work through should not be a decision the user makes, the same as stopping: whether the task was sent back or passed is read from the user's messages." The records agree: after a pressed 放行 card your next line was mostly about something else (「电脑磁盘的系统数据怎么特别大」, 「这个端点的 qwen-audio-3.0-tts-plus 支持哪些语音」) — the press was a chore on the way; and what you wanted changed you already said in words.

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **交付不出卡片，等你的话 / A hand-over gets no card; it waits on your word.** 原来要落到放行/退回卡上的交付——没人审、也没有你写的或确认过的检查撑着的文件，一段话，整理跳读成做完的，审查者和做的 Bot 同一个模型或没给依据的「通过」，你说过两次或关于画面、却没东西撑着的要求，大活的样片和最后一件——都不贴卡片、不发通知，在下一拍记成等你（`awaiting`，工作记录 `submission.awaiting_word`，一份交付一次）。球在你：看板写「等你一句话：说可以就通过，挑毛病就返工」。交了什么，是做它的 Bot 在回复里告诉你的。 / What used to come to the approve/reject card — a file hand-over nobody reviews and no check you wrote or confirmed backs, words handed over, the organizer's reading, a reviewer's approve on the producer's own model or with no evidence, required items you said twice or about the picture with nothing behind them, a large job's sample and last part — gets no card and no notification: at the next tick it is recorded as waiting on you (`awaiting`, work event `submission.awaiting_word`, once per hand-over). The ball is yours, and the board says "Waiting on your word: say it is fine, or what to fix". The Bot that made it has told you what it handed over.

2. **你的下一句话就是表态 / What you say next is your verdict.** 读你的话（[ADR 0055](0055-lines-read-by-a-model.md)）的那一次，同时给读句看你说话的地方正等你表态的交付（这件事的会话；私聊里只有这个 Bot 交的；最新 6 份，各带谁交的、哪件事哪张任务、交了什么、多久以前、是不是样片），由它说出这句话放行了哪几份（`passes`）。算放行：认可（「可以」「不错」「就这样」），拿它往下用或叫做下一步（「发给他们吧」「接着做下一集」），以及在私聊里没提它就说起了别的事——东西已经在你面前，你往下走了。不算：挑毛病（那是返工，照 ADR 0070）；要改它、给它加东西（Bot 会交新的一版，新版取代它）；问它本身的情况；只问进度、叫停、继续；只应一声、说要先看看；群里对别的 Bot 说的话。样片只有明确认可或叫它往下做才算。一句话里既挑毛病又放行同一份，按挑毛病。没有词表顶替：读不了（没配模型、出错、超时）就什么都不放行。 / The reading of your line (ADR 0055) is also shown the hand-overs waiting on you where you speak (the job's conversations; in a direct only that Bot's; the latest six, each with who handed it over, the job and ticket, what was handed over, how long ago, and whether it is a sample), and names those your line lets through (`passes`). A nod (「可以」「不错」「就这样」), using it or asking for the next step (「发给他们吧」「接着做下一集」), and in your direct going on to something else without a word about it — it is in front of you and you moved on — let it through. A fault found does not (that is rework, as in ADR 0070), nor a change or an addition to it (the Bot hands over a new version, which supersedes it), a question about it, a status question, a stop or a go on, a bare acknowledgement or "let me look", or in a group a line to another Bot. A sample takes a clear yes or a call to go on. A line that finds fault with a hand-over and nods to it sends it back. No word list stands in: with no reading (no model, an error, a timeout) nothing is let through.

3. **说出来的放行和按下去的一样算 / A 放行 said counts as one pressed.** 照卡片上按「放行」的规矩：重跑检查，门禁没过就退回生产者，还没跑完就先等它跑完再定；没东西撑着的要求算做到了；审查者的「通过」随之生效；工作记录记成你的（`submission.approved`，`by: user`，带你那句话的 `message_id`），流程图上那句话就是你的一张「放行」决定卡。你那句话下面写着结果：「已放行：…」「检查没过，已退回：…」或「等检查跑完再放行：…」，没有撤销：读错了，你说哪里不对，它照 ADR 0070 转回返工（已通过的也能转回，已交付的事重新打开）。 / As 放行 on a card always went: the checks run again — a failing gate sends it back to its producer, one not run yet is waited for first; the required items with nothing behind them count as met; a reviewer's approve it waited on takes effect; it is logged as yours (`submission.approved`, `by: user`, with your line's `message_id`), and on the trace your line becomes a 放行 decision card. Under your line it says what came of it — 「已放行：…」, 「检查没过，已退回：…」 or 「等检查跑完再放行：…」 — with no undo: if it was misread, say what is wrong and it goes back to rework as in ADR 0070 (approved work can be sent back, a delivered job reopens).

4. **Bot 正为你后来的话干活时，你的话不算对旧版的 / While its Bot works on a later line of yours, your words are not about the old version.** 你在交付等你时又说了这件事（「把猫改成橘猫」），做它的 Bot 正为这句话干活（ADR 0058 §15、§18 的同一个判断），这期间读句看不到那份旧交付；新的一版交上来就取代它，等你的是新的。 / When you said more about the job while a hand-over waited (「把猫改成橘猫」) and its Bot is at work on that line (the same test as ADR 0058 §15 and §18), the reading is not shown the old hand-over meanwhile; the new version supersedes it and is what waits on you.

5. **卡片的时机规则拿掉 / The rules for when a card went up are gone.** 没有卡片可贴，ADR 0058 §15–18 的「收起卡片、做完再贴」「Bot 还在干活先不贴」（`stillAtWork`、`submission.card_deferred`）「这次没交新的一版」「这是几点交的那一版」都去掉了；`superviseSubmissions` 不再返回卡片。 / With no card to put up, ADR 0058 §15–18's take-down-and-put-up-again, "not while its Bot is at work" (`stillAtWork`, `submission.card_deferred`), "handed over nothing new this time" and "the version handed over at…" are gone; `superviseSubmissions` returns no cards.

6. **样片的花费由交样片的 Bot 说 / The sample's sums are told by the Bot that hands it over.** 原来写在样片卡上的「样片做了约几分钟、模型费、外部调用几次，按它粗算剩下几件要多少」，现在随 `submit` 的结果交给这个 Bot（`sample`），工具说明叫它在回复里照实告诉你。 / What the sample's card said — minutes, model spend and generating calls it took, and what the rest would take at that size — now comes back to its Bot with `submit`'s result (`sample`), whose description tells it to say so to you.

## 不变 / Unchanged

- 一次做完的、日程的、有你确认过的检查撑着的、另一个模型带依据审过的交付，照旧直接放行，不等你。 / Work made in one go, a routine's runs, hand-overs backed by a check you confirmed and those another model reviewed with evidence are still let through at once.
- 退回照 ADR 0070：读出挑毛病就转回返工，你那句话下面有撤销。 / Sending back stays ADR 0070's: a line read as finding fault sends the work back, with an undo under it.
- 以前贴出、还没按的放行/退回卡照样能按；你的话放行了它对应的交付时，卡片写「已放行。」收起按钮。 / Approve/reject cards already out still take their presses; one whose hand-over your word let through says 「已放行。」 and loses its buttons.
- 动作的批准卡（危险命令、工作区外读写、新端点）、能力天花板卡、教训卡不变。 / Approval cards for actions (risky commands, outside the workspace, new endpoints), the ceiling card and the lesson card are unchanged.

## 取舍 / Trade-offs

- **你一直不说，它就一直等。** 等它的任务（依赖它的、大活里等样片的）不开工，事不交付；看板上写着在等你的话，没有通知，也不提醒。 / **If you never say anything, it waits.** Tickets that depend on it, and a large job's other parts, do not start and the job is not delivered; the board says it waits on your word, with no notification and no reminder.
- **在私聊里说起别的事就算放行。** 想过后再看，就说一句「我晚点看」；放行之后发现不对，照样说出来转回返工。 / **Moving on in your direct lets it through.** To look later, say so; found wrong after it went through, say so and it goes back.
- **读错的放行真的放行了**，没有撤销按钮：已交付的事会被你的下一句挑毛病重新打开，8 级的完工复盘可能已经跑过。 / **A misread nod really lets it through**, with no undo button: the next line finding fault reopens a delivered job, and at level 8 the retrospective may already have run.
- **审查者的结论和没东西撑着的要求不再摆在你面前**：它们跟着交付存着、放行时照旧生效，要看去交付明细。 / **A reviewer's verdict and the unbacked requirements are no longer put in front of you**: they stay with the hand-over and take effect as before; the submission details show them.

## 实现 / Implementation

`store/your-word.ts`：`waitingOnYourWord`（读句看到的候选：开着的、等你或还没被下一拍接手的交付，不含审查者正在审的；按 `jobConversations` 和私聊成员限在你说话的地方；去掉 `workOnYourLine` 为真的；最新 6 份）、`passByYourWord`（只认模型读法；在 `waitingOnYourWord` 里再核一次；`awaiting.line_id`，门禁没跑完置 `pending` 交引擎跑；否则 `resolveApproval`）。`store/review-cards.ts`：`awaitYourWord`、`markPassedLine`（`control.kind = "passed"`，按各交付此刻的状态重写结果）；`askApproval` 和卡片措辞删去。`store/submissions.ts`：`takeUpAwaiting` 与 `reviewSubmission` 改调 `awaitYourWord`；删 `holdForWork`、`holdForYourLine` 和 `store/hand-over-rest.ts`；`approve(..., line)` 记 `message_id` 并写标记，`failOnGates` 也写。`line-reading.ts`／`prompts/reader.ts`：`UserLineReading.passes`、`handed_over` 输入和第 5 项；`reader.ts` 读你的话时带上候选，这次读句的调用记录（[ADR 0083](0083-flow-draws-the-apps-calls.md)）多记 `passes`，流程里那一步写「读成：放行 N 份交付」。`engine/submissions.ts`：`noteComplaint` 改名 `noteYourWord`，先退回再放行，`pending` 的跑完门禁后 `takeUpPendingApproval`；`submit` 结果带 `sample`。`store/hand-over-outcomes.ts`：等你的话也算 `waiting_on_user`。`store/task-trace.ts`：带 `message_id` 的 `submission.approved` 成为你那句话上的放行决定。协议 `MessageControl` 加 `passed`；信使 `controlBar` 只显示结果；看板「等你一句话」。测试用的读句替身把单说「可以」「行」「不错」读成放行。

## 缺口 / Not done

- 一句话既转回了一份、又放行了另一份时，你那句话下面只写转回返工。 / A line that sent one hand-over back and let another through is marked with the send-back only.
- 群里「说起别的事」不算放行，只有明确认可或叫它往下做才算。 / In a group, moving on does not count; only a clear nod or a call to go on does.
- 读句读不了时你的话放行不了什么，也不告诉你；再说一次就好。 / With no reading, your line lets nothing through and nothing says so; say it again.
