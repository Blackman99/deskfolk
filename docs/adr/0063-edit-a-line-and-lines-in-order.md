# 改一句发出去的话，连发的几句按顺序 / Changing a line you sent, and lines taken in order

Status: implemented 2026-10-06, at every engine level with work items. The daemon says so in its snapshot (`linesInOrder`, `messageEdits`); a window or phone on an older daemon offers neither. Builds on [ADR 0040](0040-agent-kernel-the-job-owns-state.md) P4a (a working Bot's inbox), [ADR 0042](0042-requirements-ledger.md) (your words and the ledger), [ADR 0055](0055-lines-read-by-a-model.md) and [ADR 0057](0057-the-job-a-line-is-about-is-read.md) (reading a line), and follows the refile of [ADR 0043](0043-work-items-and-attribution.md) (`refileMessage`) for who hears of a change.

2026-10-06，你要两件事：改一句已经发出去的话，和连着发好几句。之前一句话发出去就改不了：打错一个数、漏一个条件，只能再补一句「上面那句改成……」，Bot 读到两句互相打架的话，转录里也一直留着错的那句。连发也卡：私聊里发出一句后，应用先读懂这句、判断它说的是哪件事（两次模型调用，中位约 3 秒，最慢 20 秒），这期间发送键是灰的；Bot 一干活，输入框下面就写着「回复结束后可发送」，其实那时能发——P4a 起这句会在它下一步读到。锁有它的原因：同一会话里的几句话各读各的，后发的一句可能先读完、先开轮，成了这一轮的触发句，先发的那句倒成了「中途收到的一句」。

On 2026-10-06 you asked for two things: to change a line you had already sent, and to send several lines in a row. A line, once sent, could not be changed: a wrong number or a missing condition could only be followed by "change the line above to…", the Bot read two lines at odds with each other, and the wrong one stayed in the transcript. Sending in a row stalled too: in a direct, after a line went out the app read it and read which job it was about (two model calls, about 3 s typically, 20 s at worst), and Send stayed grey meanwhile; while the Bot worked, the hint under the box said "Send after the reply ends" when in fact you could — since P4a the line is read at the Bot's next step. The lock had a reason: lines in one conversation were each read on their own, so a later one could finish first and open the turn as its trigger, and the earlier one became a line heard in the middle of it.

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **连发按到达顺序 / Lines in a row are taken in order.** 同一会话里你的话排成一队（`engine/intake.ts`）：后一句等前一句读完、归好、送到——它开出的轮已经在了，后一句就进那一轮的收件箱，下一步一起读到——再开始自己的读句；读归属时也看得到前一句归在哪。等待从前一句真正开始处理算起，最多 45 秒，过了照常往下走；停机时谁都不等。纯叫停、纯继续（按固定规则当场照办的）不排队，最先办；模型读出来的叫停或进度询问照样排队，正好停到前一句开出的那一轮。Bot 的话不排队。私聊因此不再在读你上一句时锁发送键；输入框下的提示改成「Enter 发送，它下一步就读到」，只有发送键真被锁时（旧守护进程）才说「回复结束后可发送」；一个已经在干活的 Bot，也不再在你后面每一句下面各画一个「正在回复」。 / Your lines in one conversation form a queue: a line waits until the one before it is read, filed and routed — the turn that one opened is then there, and this line goes into its inbox, read at its next step — before its own reading starts, and its filing read sees where the line before it went. The wait counts from when the line ahead started and lasts at most 45 s; shutting down, nobody waits. A line that is only a stop or a go on by the fixed rules skips the queue and is carried out first; a stop or status question only a model reads waits its turn, which stops the turn the line before it opened. A Bot's lines do not queue. So a direct no longer locks Send while your last line is read; the hint says "Enter to send — it reads it at its next step", and "Send after the reply ends" only where Send really waits (an older daemon); a Bot already at work is no longer drawn replying under each line you send meanwhile.

2. **改一句发出去的话 / Changing a line you sent.** 只有你写的普通话能改。不能改的：Bot 的话；你对提问的回答（一次提问只答一次）；批注合成的那条（改批注）；应用当场办掉的叫停、继续或进度询问（`messages.taken_as`：`app` 或 `answer`，撤销后交回的那句清掉，又能改）；归档的会话、Bot↔Bot 私聊、Bot 已删的私聊里的话。没有时间限制。入口在气泡上：悬停条和右键/长按菜单里的「编辑」，输入框为空时按 ↑ 改最近一句。气泡原地变成编辑框，Enter 保存、Shift+Enter 换行、Esc 取消，输入法选字的回车不算保存。改过的话时间旁标「已编辑」，点开列出之前的版本（`message_edits`，清空会话随转录一起清掉）。`PATCH /v1/messages/:id`（`{ body }`，回执去重）、`GET /v1/messages/:id/versions`，本机和手机都有。 / Only a plain line of yours can be changed — not a Bot's, not your answer to a question (a question is answered once), not a batch of annotations (change the annotations), not a stop, go on or status question the app carried out itself (`messages.taken_as`, cleared when you undo one and it goes on as a line), and not in an archived conversation, a Bot↔Bot direct, or a direct whose Bot is gone. There is no time limit. The way in is on the bubble: Edit on the hover bar and in the right-click or long-press menu, and ↑ in an empty composer for your newest line. The bubble turns into the editor: Enter saves, Shift+Enter starts a new line, Esc cancels, and the Enter that confirms an input method's candidate is no save. A changed line says Edited beside its time, which opens what it said before (`message_edits`, cleared with the transcript). `PATCH /v1/messages/:id` (`{ body }`, deduplicated by its receipt) and `GET /v1/messages/:id/versions`, on the Mac and the phone alike.

3. **谁知道你改了 / Who hears of it.** 在改字的同一次提交里定，照改归属的做法（`store/message-edits.ts`）。还没读到这句的——排着队的、被叫停扣着的收件——收件里的字直接换成新的：它只读到新版，不另说「改了」（一份收件后面拼着「附件：」行、或前面带着改归属提示的，换掉开头或结尾的那句原文）。已经读到的——读过这句的收件、以它为触发句开出的轮——各收到一条你的话：「你改了这句。现在是：「…」（原来是：「…」）」，新字在前；那一轮还在干活就在下一步读到，和中途收到的改动一样推迟这一步还没跑的调用；轮已结束就把它在这件事上的工作排进队，开新一轮来读；叫停覆盖时扣着，解除后送到。它算你的话：编号是 `U…`，要在 end_turn 里处置。叫停下的只读答复轮不算读过：解除时这句原样交回，那时已是新字。还在读的那句没有读者，读完按新字送出——排队等到号后重读一次，读句期间被改过的不当控制句办。没人收到过的话（群里都旁观了）改了也不叫醒谁。 / Decided in the write that changes the words, as a refile is. A Bot that has not read the line yet — a queued item, or one a stop holds — gets the new words in that item and nothing else (a copy with its files listed after the words, or a refile's note before them, has the old words swapped where they open or close it). A Bot that has read it — an item read, or a turn the line opened — gets a line of yours, "You changed this line. It now reads: “…” (it was: “…”)", new words first: a turn still working reads it at its next step and, like any change arriving mid-step, puts off the calls still waiting; a turn that ended has its work on the job queued, and a new turn opens to read it; a stop holds it until it lifts. It is your line: it has a `U…` id and must be disposed of in end_turn. A read-only answer under a stop has not read it: lifting the stop hands the line back as it then reads. A line still being read has no reader yet, and goes out in its new words — the intake reads it again once its turn in the queue comes, and a line changed while it was read is not carried out as a stop. A line nobody took up (every Bot in a group sat it out) wakes nobody when changed.

4. **你的原话只增不改 / Your words gain, never change.** 改动的那几小句（`changedWords`，和流程图编辑同一个读法）另存一条原话，指向这句的第一条原话（`user_quotes.edit_of`），归在这句所在的事上；只删了字就不另存——旧字还在。书记员按先后读这句的每一条原话、各读一次，所以只读到改动的部分，没改的要求不会被算成「又说了一次」；按你的话加的检查也把一句话的几条原话算作一个出处。旧原话和站在上面的台账条目都不动：改了数字的，照台账规则生成一条待你确认的替代；生效检查的数变了，照 ADR 0058 #5 出替换卡；删掉的字所在的条目照样生效，要退就在流程图上退。Bot 的局面里，「用户在这件事里的话」把它写成「在X改了一句」；转录里改过的话标着「（发出后改过）」。 / The clauses you changed (`changedWords`, as a board edit reads them) are kept as one more quote, pointing at the line's first quote (`user_quotes.edit_of`) and filed where the line is; taking words out keeps nothing new — the old words are still there. The scribe reads every quote of the line, oldest first and each once, so it reads only what changed, and a requirement you left alone is not counted as said again; checks from your words count a line's quotes as one source. The old quote, and the ledger entries standing on it, stay: a changed number makes a replacement awaiting your confirmation, as the ledger's rules have it, and a gate in force whose number changed gets a replacement card (ADR 0058 #5); an entry on words you deleted stays in force until you retire it on the board. In the Bot's situation the words you changed read "changed a line in X"; in its transcript the line is marked "（发出后改过）".

5. **不重读 / Not read again.** 改过的话不当叫停、继续或进度询问办，不重新判投诉返工，不改归属（要改用「改归属…」），不叫醒新点名的人，不改以这句命名的事。读句和归属的缓存清掉，之后读到它的按新字读。 / A changed line is not carried out as a stop, a go on or a status question, not judged again as a complaint, not refiled (use Change attribution), wakes nobody it now names, and renames no job named after it. Its cached readings are dropped, so whatever reads it later reads the new words.

6. **按停止之后改，就是你的下一句 / A change after Stop is your next line**（2026-10-09 补记 / addendum）。你按停止（等你下一句就解除的那种叫停），再改它停下时在做的那句话：改动只写进收件、被叫停扣着，可改动不是「下一句」，停止就一直不解除，Bot 一直停着——停止不在侧栏横幅里，也没有解除按钮，只能再打一句。现在改动和新的一句一样：扣着这句副本或改动收件的停止（改之前按下的）随之解除，Bot 开新一轮，先读「你改了这句」，照改后的话接着做（`stop/go-on.ts` `liftOnYourChange`，在 `noteEdited` 派发之前）。只解除扣着它的停止，按改动本身认，不按新字点名谁；要你自己解除的叫停照旧扣着，第 3 条那句「叫停覆盖时扣着」只对它们成立。 / You press Stop (a stop that lifts at your next line), then change the line it was working on: the change went into the inbox, held by the stop, but a change is no next line, so the Stop never lifted and the Bot stayed stopped — and a Stop is in no sidebar banner and has no lift button, so only typing another line got it going. Now a change counts as a new line does: the Stops holding a copy of the line or its change note (pressed before the change) lift, and the Bot opens a turn that reads "You changed this line" first and goes on from the change (`stop/go-on.ts` `liftOnYourChange`, before `noteEdited` dispatches). Only the Stops holding it lift — by the change itself, not by whom the new words name; a stop that stays until you lift it still holds it, and #3's "a stop holds it until it lifts" now means those.

7. **停止之后你别的话也是下一句 / After Stop, your other words are your next line too**（2026-10-09 再补 / second addendum）。同一个毛病还有两处：Bot 干活时你补了一句、它还没读到你就按了停止，这句被扣着，「直接插入」是灰的；按停止之后在交付卡上「退回」，返工被扣着。两处都只能再打一句话。现在「直接插入」被你按的停止扣着的那句，解除扣着它的停止并马上开一轮读它（`liftOnYourInsert`；要你自己解除的叫停照旧 409 `held`，这句下面说一声）；「退回」解除扣着那个 Bot 在这件事上的工作的停止，它带着你写的返工（`liftOnSendBack`）。另有一处更底层：停止结束一轮时，这一轮没读的话被扣着，它们的工作留在空闲，停止解除后只回到「排队中」，没有谁派出去——现在收件从扣着回到排队时，它的工作照一轮结束时的规矩排进队（`refreshHeldInbox`），按钮解除后马上派。回答阻塞的工作提问照旧不解除停止（`work-answer` 的有意设计）——同一天由 [ADR 0071](0071-a-stop-is-only-stop-for-now.md) 推翻：你的每一种叫停都只是先停下，回答提问也越过它。 / The same flaw had two more ways in: a line you sent while the Bot worked, Stop pressed before it read it, was held with Insert now off; and Send back on a hand-over's card after Stop left the rework held. Both waited for a typed line. Now Insert now on a line a Stop of yours holds lifts the Stops holding it and opens a turn on it (`liftOnYourInsert`; a stop that stays until you lift it still answers 409 `held`, said under the line), and Send back lifts the Stops on that Bot's work in the job, so it reworks with your note (`liftOnSendBack`). One cause sat deeper: a turn a Stop ended left its unread lines held and their work idle, so once the stop lifted they only went back to queued with nothing to dispatch them — now an item going from held to queued has its work queued as at a turn's end (`refreshHeldInbox`), and a button's lift dispatches at once. Answering a blocked work question still lifts no stop (`work-answer`, by design) — overruled the same day by [ADR 0071](0071-a-stop-is-only-stop-for-now.md): every stop of yours is only stop for now, and your answer goes on past it too.

## 取舍 / Trade-offs

- **第二句晚几秒到 Bot。** 它要等第一句读完；可它读归属时看得到第一句归在哪，不和第一句抢读句的两个名额，而 Bot 本来就在下一步才读它。 / The second line reaches the Bot a few seconds later: it waits for the first to be read; in return its filing read sees where the first went, it does not compete with the first for the two reading slots, and the Bot reads it at its next step either way.
- **删掉的要求不自动退。** 和「离开生效只有你自己操作」一致；代价是改完再到流程图上退一次。 / A requirement you deleted is not retired by itself, in keeping with "only you take an entry out of force"; the cost is a second step on the board.
- **纠正算你的话。** Bot 必须处置它，一轮里多一条要回应的收件；换来的是它不会悄悄照旧版做完。 / A correction is a line of yours: the Bot must dispose of it, one more item in its turn, so that it cannot quietly finish on the old words.

## 缺口 / Not done

- **iPhone 上没有编辑入口**：Safari 不出长按菜单（回复、复制也一样）。Android 和桌面有。 / No way to edit on an iPhone: Safari opens no long-press menu (reply and copy share the gap). Android and the desktop have it.
- **一轮可能瞥见还没路由的那句**：它在转录窗里读到过旧字，之后读到新字时没有「你改了」提示。 / A turn may have glimpsed a line not yet routed in its transcript window, and later reads the new words with no note that they changed.
- **上一条还在路上时仍不能发下一条**（手机经中继、或附件上传中）；你的话下面「Bot 读到没有」仍不画（2026-10-08 起还没读到的话下面有一行，能撤回或直接插入：[ADR 0069](0069-take-back-or-insert-a-line-not-read-yet.md)）。 / Send still waits while the last line is on its way (over the relay, or uploading files); whether a Bot has read a line of yours is still not drawn under it (from 2026-10-08 a line not read yet has a row under it, and can be taken back or inserted now: [ADR 0069](0069-take-back-or-insert-a-line-not-read-yet.md)).

## 2026-10-10 补记：删掉的字，提醒你退不退 / Words you took out are put to you

「删掉的字所在的条目照样生效，要退在流程图上退」留下了一个没人看得见的口子：只删字的改动连书记员都不叫，旧条目就一直生效。IG MV 那件事（2026-10-09 00:54）你发出后一分钟把「如果自己整不了模型就改成 2D 动画风格…也可以」删掉，R275 仍然生效，和换成的 3D 方向并列。现在每次改动（只删了字的也算）书记员都再读一次：给它看改前改后，和这句话早先的字记下、还在台账里、你没在别的话里再说过的条目（只按结构挑，不比字），它在答案里用 `withdraws` 指出被你改掉的那几条。应用不替你退：这几条标上 `withdraw_edit_id`，在要点「你的要求」最前面列成「你改掉了的字」，你按「不再适用」退掉，或按「留着」不再问（`requirement.withdraw_dismissed`）。「离开生效只有你自己操作」不变。

"An entry on words you deleted stays in force until you retire it on the board" left a gap nobody could see: a change that only took words out did not even reach the scribe, so the entry simply stayed in force. On the IG MV job (2026-10-09 00:54) you took "if you can't build the models, a 2D animated style like the League theme MVs is fine too" out of a line a minute after sending it, and R275 stayed in force beside the 3D direction that replaced it. Every change now goes to the scribe again, one that only took words out included: it is shown the line before and after and the entries that stand on the line's earlier words, are still in the ledger and were not said again in another line (picked by structure, no comparison of words), and names in `withdraws` those your change took back. The app retires nothing for you: they are marked `withdraw_edit_id` and listed first in the plan's "What you asked for" as "Words you took out", where "No longer holds" retires one and "Keep it" stops asking (`requirement.withdraw_dismissed`). "Only you take an entry out of force" stands.
