# 你自己的环节：在应用已有的时机上，按你的规矩判 / Steps of your own: your rules, at the moments the app already has

Status: implemented 2026-11-04. Carries out what [ADR 0085](0085-engines.md) left for later ("custom steps, effects from a fixed list only") and the fourth step [ADR 0103](0103-an-engine-can-work-in-no-jobs.md) set aside until its first three had been used.

2026-11-04 你问怎么让引擎完全由你定义，而不是现在这样固定的环节。到这一天，引擎能开关二十个内置环节、改它们的数、带自己的一份系统指令、少给几组工具，也能整个不跑事（「自由」）；但加不了一个应用没写的环节，比如「写完 TS 跑一遍 lint」「收尾前跑测试，不过就退回」「不许动 `.env`」「交上来之前照我的规矩看一遍」。查下来三处挡着：保存时不认识的环节 id 被拒，读回时被悄悄丢掉；守护进程没有通用的环节执行器，约二十处按名字问某个环节开不开；分工页和流程图按固定 id 查文案，新 id 会直接报错。

On 2026-11-04 you asked how an engine could be wholly yours to define rather than the fixed steps it has. By then an engine could switch twenty built-in steps and change their numbers, carry a System section of its own and offer fewer tools, and leave out jobs altogether (Plain); it could not add a step the app does not ship, such as "lint TypeScript after a write", "run the tests before a turn ends and send it back when they fail", "never touch `.env`", or "check what is handed in against my rules". Three things stood in the way: a step id the app did not know was refused on save and dropped on read; the daemon had no runner for steps in general, only some twenty places asking for one by name; and Roles and the flow looked steps up in fixed copy, so a new id broke them.

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **时机是应用的，环节是你的 / The moments are the app's; the steps are yours.** 一个环节挂在应用已有的六个时机之一：有副作用的调用之前、之后，一轮收尾时，交上来时，交付放行后，你退回后。时机就是应用的生命周期；你的环节挂在内置环节旁边，不重排、不替换内核，底线（ADR 0103 决定 4）和一件件事的状态机（决定 3）照旧。要一套完全不同的生命周期，答案是以「自由」为底，再加你自己的环节。 / A step hangs on one of six moments the app already has: before a call with an effect, after one, as a turn ends, when work is handed in, after a hand-over is approved, after you send one back. The moments are the app's life cycle; your steps hang beside its own, reordering and replacing nothing of the kernel, and the floor (ADR 0103 decision 4) and the job kernel's state machine (decision 3) stay as they are. For a wholly different life cycle, build on Plain and add your steps.

2. **「不过」做什么只能从固定清单里选 / What a "no" does comes from a fixed list.** 环节只判断过不过、为什么；拒绝、退回、拦交付、告诉你都由应用自己的代码做（ADR 0040：模型和命令负责读、判、提议，状态只由确定性代码写）。 / A step only judges, pass or not and why; refusing, sending back, holding a hand-over and telling you are the app's own code (ADR 0040: models and commands read, judge and propose; deterministic code writes state).

   | 时机 / Moment | 能选的 / What a "no" can do | 需要一件件事 / Needs jobs |
   |---|---|---|
   | 调用之前 `before_effect` | 拒掉这次调用，理由给 Bot；或放行，把输出附给 Bot / refuse the call, the reason to the Bot; or let it through with the output beside it | 否 / no |
   | 调用之后 `after_effect` | 附在调用结果旁边给 Bot / beside the call's result, to the Bot | 否 / no |
   | 一轮收尾时 `turn_end` | 退回接着做；或告诉你 / send back to go on; or tell you | 否 / no |
   | 交上来时 `handed_in` | 像没过的检查一样退回 / sent back as a failed check would | 是 / yes |
   | 交付放行后 `accepted` | 告诉你 / tell you | 是 / yes |
   | 你退回后 `overturned` | 告诉你 / tell you | 是 / yes |

   「调用」指写文件、删文件、命令和 MCP 工具（Claude Code 的 Edit/Write 按 `write_file` 算）。「告诉你」是会话里一条对 Bot 隐藏的系统行，排在它说的事后面，和结束契约告诉你的那种一样：不替 Bot 说话，只因为你挂了这一步才出现。需要一件件事的时机在不跑事的引擎里不会来，环节画成灰的、写明原因，你设的值留着。 / A call is a write, a delete, a command or an MCP tool (Claude Code's Edit and Write count as `write_file`). Telling you is a line in the conversation that no Bot reads, after what it is about, as the end contract tells you things: it speaks for no Bot, and is there only because you hung the step. A moment that needs jobs never comes in an engine with none; the step is drawn faded with why, its settings kept.

3. **三种判法 / Three ways to judge.** 规则：只看命中，命中就算不过（「不许动 `**/.env`」），确定、零成本。命令：退出码 0 算过，否则输出的末尾当理由；它在工作区里跑，环境变量和验收检查的一样干净，stdin 收到一份 JSON（时机、调用、路径、回复、交上来的文件、会话和 Bot），另有 `DESKFOLK_EVENT`、`DESKFOLK_STEP`、`DESKFOLK_PATHS`（一行一个）、`DESKFOLK_COMMAND`、`DESKFOLK_WORKSPACE`；碰到工作区外的命令不跑。模型：你写的规矩加上发生了什么，问它过不过、为什么，答案的格式锁着。 / A rule: matching alone is a no ("never touch `**/.env`"), certain and free. A command: exit 0 passes, anything else fails with its output's tail as the reason; it runs in the workspace with the acceptance checks' clean environment, reads a JSON of what happened on stdin (the moment, the call, its paths, the reply, the files handed in, the conversation and Bot) and gets `DESKFOLK_EVENT`, `DESKFOLK_STEP`, `DESKFOLK_PATHS` (one a line), `DESKFOLK_COMMAND` and `DESKFOLK_WORKSPACE`; one that reaches outside the workspace does not run. A model: your rule and what happened, asked whether it passes and why, its answer's shape locked.

4. **看哪些只用结构化字段 / What a step looks at is structured only.** 工具种类（只在调用前后）和相对工作区的路径 glob：调用前后是这次调用的文件，收尾时是这一轮写过的文件，交上来和之后是交付里的文件。凡是要看「这句话说的是什么」的条件都交给模型环节，不加词表（2026-10-03 起的规矩）。 / Kinds of tool (around a call only) and path globs relative to the workspace: around a call its files, as a turn ends the files it wrote, on a hand-in and after it the files handed in. Anything that turns on what words say is a model step's, never a word list (the rule since 2026-10-03).

5. **判不了就放行 / A step that cannot judge lets things through.** 超时、起不来、模型没答或答案读不出：记成出错，画在流程图上，什么都不拦，和验收检查的出错、被拦在工作区外同一个意思。 / Timed out, would not start, a model that did not answer or whose answer could not be read: an error on the flow that holds nothing, as a check's error or one blocked outside the workspace.

6. **退回和结束契约共用两次 / A send-back shares the end contract's two.** 收尾时的退回占这一段两次退回里的一次（和结束契约自己的规矩共用，记成 `end.rejected`，代码 `custom_step`），用完了这一轮照常收尾、告诉你哪一步还是没过。这是防打转，不是轮次上限：一轮干多久都行（2026-10-17）。一句回复要被退回时先发出去，Bot 再听到退回；只在你说完成或回答了的结尾上判，Bot 说卡住了或放弃时不判。调用之前的拒绝记成 `step_refused`，在效果账本里算被拒，不算这一轮的失败。 / A send-back as a turn ends is one of the segment's two (shared with the contract's own rules, logged as `end.rejected` with code `custom_step`); with none left the turn ends as it would and you are told which step still failed. That keeps a rule from going round for good; it is no cap on a turn, which runs as long as it works (2026-10-17). A reply about to be sent back goes out first and the Bot hears it after; only an ending that says done or answered is judged, not one saying it is blocked or gave up. A refusal before a call is `step_refused`, refused in the effect ledger and no failure of the turn.

7. **跑道和超时 / Lanes and timeouts.** 调用前后的命令不排进验收检查那条全应用一次一个的跑道（那会卡住所有 Bot 的调用），各自在这一轮里跑，默认 10 秒，最长 60 秒；收尾时和交上来默认 120 秒，最长 600 秒，交上来的和检查共用跑道；放行后和退回后在调度器的下一拍里跑，不挡任何东西。模型环节用调用自己的时限。 / Commands around a call do not join the checks' daemon-wide lane, one at a time (it would hold every Bot's calls), and run within the turn, 10 s by default and at most 60; at a turn's end and on a hand-in 120 s by default and at most 600, a hand-in's in the checks' lane; after an approval or a send-back on the scheduler's next tick, holding nothing up. A model step takes its call's own limit.

8. **交上来的环节能拦、不能放 / A hand-in step can hold back, never let through.** 它和绑定的检查一起跑，没有绑定检查的交付也跑；结论在交付结算时和检查放在一起读，不过就和检查没过一样退回、算进能力天花板。它不算「你的」检查：你的规矩能把一份交付挡回去，不能替没人担保的交付放行（ADR 0084 的放行不变）。 / It runs beside the bound checks, and on a hand-over with none; its verdict is read with them where the hand-over settles, a fail sending it back as a failed check does and counting towards the ceiling. It is not counted as a check of yours: your rule can hold a hand-over back, never let one through that nobody else stands behind (ADR 0084's approvals are unchanged).

9. **放行后和退回后 / After an approval and after a send-back.** 放行有好几条路（你说、审查的 Bot、应用按检查、日程），所以不在每处挂，而是调度器每拍从工作记录里读新的放行和你的退回，书签存在设置里、不通知窗口；第一次读从当时开始，之前的不补跑。 / Approvals come by several ways (your word, the reviewing Bot, the app on its checks, a routine), so the steps are not hung at each; the scheduler's tick reads new approvals and your send-backs from the work log, from a bookmark kept in settings that no window is told about. The first look starts from then; nothing before it is run.

10. **新的内置调用「自定义环节」 / A new built-in call, `custom_step`.** 模型环节跑在分工页上它自己的模型上，没选就跟看图判定；它挂在调用前后时每次调用都要等，你可以给它配一个快而便宜的。它外面那层提示词（`call.custom_step`）和别的内置提示词一样能改，答案格式锁着。花费记成 `step`（类别 `organize`）。只用 Claude Code 装好的判定不看它，否则升级后已经装好的人会被送回向导。 / A model step runs on its own model on Roles, else the picture judge's; hung around a call it holds every such call, so you may give it a fast, cheap one. The frame around your rule (`call.custom_step`) is editable as the other built-in prompts are, its answer shape locked. Spend is `step` (kind `organize`). The Claude-only setup test passes it over, or everyone set up that way would be sent back through the wizard.

11. **只有你能写 / Only you write them.** 存在引擎的差别里（`custom_steps`，每个引擎最多 12 个），修改记录照旧，以它为底另建时一起带走。Bot 没有改引擎的工具；以后 Bot 提议改引擎时也不能写命令。手机端能写，和命令类验收检查一致：它是你配对过的设备。整份差别最多 200,000 字，本机和手机同一个上限。 / They live in the engine's difference (`custom_steps`, at most 12 an engine) with its revisions, and go along when you build on it. Bots have no tool to change an engine, and will not write commands when they come to propose engine changes. Your phone may write them, as it may write command checks: it is a device you paired. A whole difference is at most 200,000 characters, on this machine and the phone alike.

12. **试一次 / Try it.** 面板上拿一个样例试草稿（`POST /v1/playbooks/:id/custom-steps/try`）：没填的取最近的（Bot 跑过的最新一条命令、最新一句回复、最新一份交付），什么都不拦、不退回、不告诉谁、不记进流程；模型环节试一次也照常记花费。它不带请求回执，可以跑一阵。 / The panel tries a draft on a sample (`POST /v1/playbooks/:id/custom-steps/try`): what is left out is the newest of its kind (the latest command a Bot ran, the latest reply, the latest hand-over); nothing is refused, sent back, told or put on the flow, and a model step's try is billed like any call. It carries no request receipt and may take a while.

13. **记录 / Records.** 每次判断一条 `call_runs`（`role = custom_step`，加 `custom_step`：哪一步、叫什么、什么时机，交上来时加 `submission_id`），命令和规则的也记，所以流程图上都看得到；同一步在一张卡下合在一起，不过的算异常、先摆出来（ADR 0094）。 / Each judgement is a `call_runs` row (`role = custom_step`, with `custom_step`: which step, its name, the moment, and `submission_id` on a hand-in), a command's and a rule's too, so the flow shows them all; a step's runs fold under one card, and a "no" counts as gone wrong and comes first (ADR 0094).

## 后果 / Consequences

- **外部 agent 事后才说的调用拦不住 / An external agent's calls the app only hears of afterwards cannot be refused.** 没有「之前」，只有调用之后的环节能看到它们；面板上写明。 / There is no "before" for them; only a step after a call sees them, and the panel says so.
- **被拒的那次调用之前，事已经绑好了 / A refused first effect has already bound its job.** 桌面段第一次有副作用的调用，先绑到一件事上、再过你的环节；环节拒掉它时，绑定不撤回，这一轮接着在那件事里做。这不是缺陷：绑定本身没有副作用，拒绝的是动作。 / A desk segment's first call with an effect is bound to a job before your steps see it; when one refuses it, the binding stays and the turn goes on in that job. That is no bug: binding has no effect of its own, and what is refused is the act.
- **降级会丢 / A downgrade drops them.** 旧版本读到 `custom_steps` 时略过，在旧版本里存一次引擎就没了；修改记录里还在。版本闸不动，没有迁移，只多了 `call_runs` 两列。 / An older build passes `custom_steps` over, so saving the engine there loses them; the revisions keep them. The version gate is untouched: no migration, two more `call_runs` columns.
- **修改记录看不到改了什么 / The history shows no before and after.** 加删一步在修改记录里只是一次「修改」，以后和撤销一起做。 / Adding or removing a step reads as one edit in the history; that comes with undo.
- **没做 / Not done.** 你的话进来时的时机（条件只能交给模型，而且每句话都要多等一次）、「先问你」这种效果（Claude Agent 那条路上已经有一处批准，再加一处就有两个来源）、群里没点名的时机、Bot 提议改引擎、按引擎改内置环节的提示词、分享和导入引擎、撤销修改记录。 / The moment your line comes in (its conditions could only be a model's, and every line would wait once more), an "ask you first" effect (Claude Agent's path already has one approval source, and this would make two), the moment of a group line naming nobody, Bots proposing engine changes, per-engine prompts for the built-in steps, sharing and importing engines, and undoing revisions.
