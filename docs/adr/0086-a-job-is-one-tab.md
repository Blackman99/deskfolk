# 一件事一个标签 / A job is one tab

Status: implemented 2026-10-12. Replaces the three tabs of a job's flow, board and plan (2026-10-10, `2407e068`); builds on ADR 0045 (who has the ball), ADR 0046 (reviewers), ADR 0074 (where windows open), ADR 0083 (the flow draws the app's own calls) and ADR 0085 (engines).

## 背景 / Context

你说（2026-10-11）：「当前任务面板 + 流程（分工配置）+ 要点的方向感觉是对的，但是这种三分离视图的表现形式始终觉得没那么好用。」两天里这三样换了三种摆法：流程旁边的侧栏，一个窗格里的切换，再到各自一个标签，仍然不好用。问题不在摆在哪，而在三个视图是同一件事的三面，却互不相干：每个标签有自己的「这件事」切换，换了一个，另两个还停在旧的那件；在看板上点了一个任务，旁边的流程不会点亮它，视图之间的跳转只是一次性的请求，打开另一个标签就用掉了。一个问题（「03 为什么卡住」）要在三处之间来回：看板看状态，要点看哪条检查没过，流程看最近几轮。

我们拿本机库里三件事（IG 纪念 MV、20 集剧本、一拳超人）画过两种做法比较。整张泳道当主视图不合适：活基本是一件接一件做的（不同任务同时在跑的，最多 12 对，多数是 0），一两条道装下了大部分轮（一拳超人 69 轮里 66 轮在 #02），按时间画大半是空白。你选了另一种：任务栏里每行一条缩略泳道，中间是现在的流程图，右边是详情栏。

同一份数据也说了分工：记下的负责人和实际干活的差得很远。一拳超人里审片员名下一个任务都没有，跑了 35 轮，33 轮在视频导演的任务上，审查者一栏却是空的；20 集剧本里故事编剧、字幕校对师名下有任务，在这件事里一轮没跑；101 个任务里设了审查者的只有 1 个。

You said (2026-10-11): "The current task board + flow (with the division of work) + plan feel like the right direction, but splitting them into three views never feels that usable." In two days the three had been laid out three ways — a rail beside the flow, a switch in one pane, a tab each — and none worked. The trouble was not where they sat but that three faces of one job did not know of each other: each tab had its own job switcher, so switching one left the other two on the old job; a ticket picked on the board did not light on the flow beside it, and going from one view to another was a one-shot request a tab used up. One question ("why is 03 stuck") meant going back and forth: the board for its status, the plan for the check that failed, the flow for its latest rounds.

We drew two ways of joining them on three jobs from this Mac's records (the IG memorial MV, the 20-episode script, One Punch Man). A full swimlane as the main view did not fit: the work runs one thing after another (tickets running at the same time: at most 12 pairs of turns, mostly none), one or two lanes hold most turns (66 of One Punch Man's 69 on #02), and drawn on a clock it is mostly empty. You chose the other: a rail of tickets with a small lane in each row, the flow as it is in the middle, and an inspector beside it.

The same records spoke to the division of work: who is written down as owning a ticket and who works it are far apart. On One Punch Man 审片员 owned nothing and ran 35 turns, 33 of them on 视频导演's tickets, with no reviewer set; on the script job 故事编剧 and 字幕校对师 owned tickets and ran no turn in the job; of 101 tickets, one had a reviewer.

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **一件事一个标签，三块 / One tab, three parts.** 标题行下面，左边任务栏，中间流程图，右边详情栏。三块共用一件事和一处选中（`JobState`）：在哪里换事，三块一起换；在哪里选中一个任务，任务栏、流程图和详情栏一起说它。任务栏和详情栏的宽度能拖，按浏览器记住。没有任务的事没有任务栏；不认识规划的旧版本只有流程图。/ Under the title row: the rail on the left, the flow in the middle, the inspector on the right. One job and one pick for all three (`JobState`): a job switched anywhere is switched everywhere, a ticket picked anywhere is the one the rail, the flow and the inspector all show. The rail's and the inspector's widths can be dragged and are kept per browser. A job with no tickets has no rail; a daemon that predates plans draws the flow alone.

2. **任务栏和缩略泳道 / The rail and its lanes.** 每个任务一行：编号、标题、负责人的头像、状态、轮数，下面一条缩略泳道。所有行共用一条轴：这件事的每张卡按先后排成一列一列，相邻两张隔了半小时以上的空档折成一道细竖条，悬停写停了多久。每一块是那一轮，颜色是那个 Bot 头像的颜色，被叫停或中断的只画边框。最上面一行是你的话，最下面一行是没归到任何任务的轮。点一行选中这个任务（再点放下）；点行里的泳道，选中它并让流程图滑到离点处最近的那张卡；上下键在行之间走。要点里点一个状态，任务栏只列这个状态的任务，直到清掉。/ One row per ticket: its number, title, its owner's face, its status and how many turns, with a small lane under it. Every row shares one axis: the job's cards in the order they happened, a column each, a silence of over half an hour folded into a thin bar that says how long it was. Each mark is a turn, in the colour of its Bot's face, an outline where it was stopped or cut. The first row is your lines, the last the turns filed under no ticket. A row picks its ticket (again puts it down); a press on its lane picks it too and slides the flow to the card nearest the press; up and down walk the rows. A status pressed in the plan lets only that status through on the rail until you clear it.

3. **看板是任务栏展开 / The board is the rail opened out.** 任务栏头上的按钮把它展开成五列看板，盖在任务栏和流程图上（流程图原地藏起，镜头不丢），照旧拖卡片换状态。看板上选中的卡片不再在卡片里展开它要满足的和设置，这些在详情栏里。所有对任务的改动——状态、审查者、模型、依赖、样片——经同一条队列发出（`TicketEdits`），看板和详情栏共用。/ A button on the rail's head opens it out into the board of five columns over the rail and the flow (the flow hidden in place, its camera kept); cards are dragged between columns as before. A card picked on the board no longer unfolds what it answers to and its settings under it: the inspector shows them. Every edit to a ticket — status, reviewer, model, what it waits for, the sample — goes out through one queue (`TicketEdits`), shared by the board and the inspector.

4. **详情栏 / The inspector.** 打开的环节优先，其次选中的任务，什么都没选是整件事的要点。环节：原来浮在流程右侧的面板搬进来，模型、提示词版本、用时、原始回答和「改这个调用」都在这里（取代 ADR 0083 2026-10-11 补记的位置）。任务：状态菜单、谁拿着球、说明、产物、「在流程里看」「跳到这一轮」「设为样片」，它要满足的和它的设置；「在要点里看」把详情栏换成按它点亮的要点，头上有回到这个任务。要点就是原来的要点页，在这一栏里排成一列；按一下展开，详情栏盖住整个标签，按原来的两栏排，任务栏和流程图原地藏起。/ An opened step first, then the picked ticket, else the whole job's plan. A step: the panel that lay over the flow's right edge moves in here, with its model, prompt version, time, raw answer and "Change this call" (superseding where ADR 0083's 2026-10-11 addendum put it). A ticket: its status menu, who has the ball, its description and files, "See it on the trace", "Jump to this turn" and "Make it the sample", what it answers to and its settings; "See in the plan" turns the inspector to the plan held to it, with a way back to the ticket on top. The plan is the plan page as it was, laid out as one column here; one press widens the inspector over the whole tab, laid out in its two columns, the rail and the flow hidden in place.

5. **按自己的宽度排 / Laid out by its own width.** 标签窄于 900px（手机、窄窗格、竖着的平板）时一次一块：标题下一行切换「任务 / 流程 / 要点」，记住你上次选的；在任务里选中一个任务、在流程里打开一个环节，都从底部拉起一张面板，手机上返回键先收起它；「在要点里看」直接切到要点。一条消息要它的卡片时，切回流程，不改你记住的那一块。宽的时候没有切换。/ Narrower than 900px (a phone, a narrow pane, a tablet upright) it is one part at a time: a switch under the title — Tickets, Trace, Plan — that remembers your choice; a ticket picked among the tickets or a step opened on the flow comes up as a sheet from the bottom, which Back on a phone puts away first; "See in the plan" turns to the plan. A message asking for its card turns to the flow without changing what you chose. Wide, there is no switch.

6. **分工 / The division of work.** 任务栏每行有负责人和审查者的头像；实际跑这个任务的 Bot 和负责人对不上时，行下写「实际：X」或「还有 X」。详情栏的整件事上面一张分工表：每个 Bot 负责哪些、审查哪些、在这件事里实际跑了几轮（其中几轮在别人的任务上、几轮没归任务），名下有任务却一轮没跑的标出来；下面是这件事的调用跑在哪套引擎上（按守护进程的读法：这件事所在的会话、它从哪个会话分出来，再是设置里的默认）、为什么是它、它没开的环节和用它的会话数；标题行有「引擎：X」，和这里的「在分工页打开」一样，打开分工页在这套引擎上。标题行还有「需要你看 N」：没过的检查、卡住的进展和等你确认的要求加起来，点一下详情栏回到整件事。任务的设置下多一行「实际跑的」；一个既不是负责人也不是审查者的 Bot 在这个任务上跑了至少 5 轮、任务还没有审查者时，给一个一键设成审查者的按钮（只在交付审查打开时），不进会话、不出卡片。调用的模型和提示词不分引擎，环节的「改这个调用」照旧说改的是这个调用本身。/ Each rail row shows its owner's and its reviewer's faces; where the Bots who ran the ticket and its owner differ, the row says "Actually: X" or "Also X". Above the whole job's plan in the inspector, a division table: what each Bot owns, reviews, and how many turns it ran in the job (how many on someone else's ticket, how many under none), those who own tickets and ran nothing marked; under it the engine the job's calls run under (read as the daemon reads it: the job's conversation, the one it was opened from, then Settings' default), why it is that one, the steps it leaves off and how many conversations run it; "Engine: X" in the title row, like "Open in Roles" here, opens Roles on that engine. The title row also says "N need you": the failed checks, blocked progress and requirements waiting for your word, one press turning the inspector back to the whole job. A ticket's settings gain a line of who actually ran it; when a Bot that is neither its owner nor its reviewer ran at least five of its turns and it has no reviewer, a button makes that Bot the reviewer (only with reviews on), in the inspector only — no line in the conversation, no card. A call's model and prompts are not per engine, so a step's "Change this call" still says the change is to the call itself.

7. **每条会话一个标签 / One tab per conversation.** 消息的右键菜单只有「这件事的流程」（停在这条消息的卡片上，选中它所在的任务）；会话行和会话标签的菜单只有「流程」。「窗口打开方式」里去掉看板、要点两行（ADR 0074 补记）。以前存下的看板、要点标签打开时，是展开的看板、按它的任务点亮的要点，再要一次就和流程标签合成一个。标签名照旧是「<会话名>流程」。/ A message's menu has "This job's flow" only (landing on the message's card, its ticket picked); a conversation's row and tab have "Flow" only. "Where windows open" loses its Board and Plan rows (ADR 0074 addendum). A board or plan tab saved before opens as the board opened out, or the plan held to its ticket, and asked for again it is the one flow tab. The tab is still named "<conversation> flow".

## 后果 / Consequences

- **整张泳道没做 / No full swimlane.** 按任务分道、按人分道的整张泳道，留给好几个 Bot 真正并行开工的大活；现在的数据里看不出它的好处。/ A full swimlane, by ticket or by Bot, is left for large jobs where several Bots really work at once; the records so far do not show it paying off.
- **一套组件，按宽度换排法 / One component, laid out by width.** 手机、平板和桌面用的是同一个 `JobLayout`；窄的判定读它自己的宽度，测试里没有宽度时，页面算窄、窗格算宽。/ The phone, the tablet and the desktop use the same `JobLayout`; narrow is read from its own width, and in tests, where there is none, a page is narrow and a pane is wide.
- **一个视图的旧链接 / Old links to one view.** 带 `view=board` 或 `view=spec` 的标签和请求照样认，作为打开时的状态，不再是一个单独的标签。/ Tabs and requests with `view=board` or `view=spec` are still read, as the state to open in, no longer as a tab of their own.
- **负责人的来历没改 / Where owners come from is unchanged.** 负责人仍是整理器按「流程与分工」先填的；分工表把差别摆出来，不替你改。/ Owners are still filled in by the organizer from the plan's process; the division table shows the difference and does not change it for you.
