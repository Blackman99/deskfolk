# 应用不插话：你的话交给 Bot，停、继续、退回只按读句 / The app stays out of the conversation: your lines go to the Bots, and stop, go on and send back only on a reading

Status: implemented 2026-10-09, at every engine level with holds (control) and submissions (send back). Builds on [ADR 0055](0055-lines-read-by-a-model.md) (lines read by a model), [ADR 0057](0057-the-job-a-line-is-about-is-read.md) (which job a line is about), [ADR 0058](0058-the-app-asks-only-when-it-needs-you.md) (the app asks only when it needs you) and [ADR 0069](0069-take-back-or-insert-a-line-not-read-yet.md). Supersedes the parts of [ADR 0040](0040-agent-kernel-the-job-owns-state.md) P2 and [ADR 0041](0041-control-plane-holds-and-restarts.md) that carry out a control line by its words, ADR 0041's cross-job lift of a go on said to a Bot, the app's own status answer (进度询问), the app's stand-in for a read-only answer that said nothing, and [ADR 0046](0046-submissions-and-reviews.md) §6.6's card asking whether to send work back.

2026-10-09 早上，你在视频导演的私聊里两次问「最终成片呢」，回你的都是应用拼出来的一段进度——挂在视频导演名下，它本人一句都没收到。读句（一次 deepseek、一次 haiku）都把这句读成「只问进度」，应用照 2026-09 的规矩自己答：那时群里每问一句「怎么样了」，都会打断干活的 Bot，有一次还开出一件叫「怎么样了」的事，自己跑了 79 段。可这一次视频导演根本没在干活，应用的进度只能写「现在没有人在做」，然后什么也不发生。接着你说「继续」：这句先被固定规则当成纯继续，在还没读、还没归到哪件事之前就照办了——解除的是视频导演 16:59 在《一拳超人》上的一条旧叫停，那件事又起不来，MV 这边什么都没动，这句也没交给它。三句话，Bot 一句没听到。你的结论：「应用插话是非常不正确的做法，应用应该尽量少的介入，不论是退回不退回，继续不继续，停止不停止，都应该读句模型上。」

On the morning of 2026-10-09 you asked 「最终成片呢」 ("where's the final cut?") twice in 视频导演's direct, and both times the answer was a status block the app put together — shown under 视频导演's name, while the Bot itself heard neither line. The reader (deepseek once, haiku once) read the line as only asking where the work stands, and the app answered it itself, by a rule from 2026-09: back then every 「怎么样了」 in a group cut a working Bot off, and once opened a job named 「怎么样了」 that ran 79 turns by itself. This time 视频导演 was not working at all, so the app's answer could only say nobody was, and nothing happened. Then you said 「继续」: the fixed rules took it for a pure go on and carried it out before it was read or filed — lifting an old stop on 视频导演's 《一拳超人》 work from 16:59, which could not start again, while nothing moved on the MV and the line never reached the Bot. Three lines; the Bot heard none. Your conclusion: the app talking in the conversation is wrong; it should step in as little as it can, and whether to send back, go on or stop is the reader model's to read.

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **你的每句话都交给 Bot，应用不替它回话 / Every line of yours goes to the Bots, and the app answers none in their place.** 只问进度的话照常交给它说给的 Bot：Bot 在干活就进它的收件箱、下一步读到（不打断，ADR 0040 P4a；ADR 0069 那行写着它在等），空着就叫醒它，它自己答。应用的进度行（`engine/status-question.ts`）删了。叫停下的只读答复一句话都没说时，应用也不再替它写「它被叫停着」。应用在对话里还写的只有两种：ADR 0058 那几张需要你的卡片，和一次叫停的回执——停下的 Bot 没法自己说它停了，回执也给撤销。「继续」不再有回执：接着做的 Bot 自己说。

   A line that only asks where the work stands goes to the Bot it is said to like any line: into its inbox when it is working, read at its next step without cutting it off (ADR 0040 P4a; ADR 0069's row says it waits), or waking it when it is not, and the Bot answers. The app's status line (`engine/status-question.ts`) is gone, and so is the app's line in place of a read-only answer that said nothing. The app still writes only two things in a conversation: the cards of ADR 0058 that need you, and a stop's receipt — a stopped Bot cannot say it stopped, and the receipt carries the undo. A go on gets no receipt: the Bot that goes on says so.

2. **停、继续只按读句 / Stop and go on only on a reading.** 固定规则不再先读、不再照办：一句话是不是叫停、是不是继续，只看读句（模型）怎么读。读句读不了（没设、出错、超时，退回词表）时什么都不执行，这句照常交给 Bot——要立刻停，用输入框的停止按钮。读句从这句到达就开始，不等它前面还在读的话：读成纯叫停的照样插到它们前面（ADR 0063 的先后只管别的话）。规则只剩两个用处：话里说的范围（「你」是谁、「所有 Bot」、「这件事」），和叫停回执要不要多给一个「作废这件事」（「算了，停吧」）——都不执行任何东西。

   The fixed rules no longer read a line first or carry anything out: whether a line stops or goes on is the reader's (a model's) to say. When it cannot read the line — none set, an error, a timeout, so the word lists read it — nothing is carried out and the line goes to the Bot as said; for a stop that cannot wait there is the composer's Stop button. The reading starts as the line arrives, not after the lines still being read before it, so a line read as nothing but a stop still goes ahead of them (ADR 0063's order holds for the rest). The rules keep two uses, neither of which carries anything out: the scope the words name (who 「你」 is, 「所有 Bot」, 「这件事」), and whether a stop's receipt also offers to drop the job (「算了，停吧」).

3. **「继续」只作用在它说的那件事上 / A go on acts on the job it is about, and only that one.** 读成继续的话（单说「继续」，或「继续做第二集，……」这样带着别的话的）先归到它说的那件事，再解除挡着它说给的那几个 Bot 做这件事的叫停：Bot 身上的、Bot 在这件事或这张任务上的、它某一段上的；这个会话自己的叫停、和你在这里叫停的这件事，只在这句话说给这里所有 Bot 时才解除。别的事上的叫停一律不动；话里点了「所有 Bot」「全都」的，才解除你建的每一条。然后这句像普通的话一样叫醒 Bot，Bot 从这句接着做、自己回你；它在别的会话里被停下的这件事的活，这句没叫醒它去做的，照旧带着一条只给它的说明在原处重新开始。归不到哪件事时，只解除 Bot 身上、不分事的叫停。还挡着的叫停（全部、别处建的整个群或整件事）不解除：Bot 收到这句只能读和回答，它被告知叫停还在、要在回执上解除，别说自己在接着做。

   A line read as a go on — 「继续」 alone, or with more in it like 「继续做第二集，……」 — is filed first, and then the stops that keep the Bots it is said to from the job it is filed under go: on the Bot, on its work in that job or on that ticket, on one of its turns; this conversation's own stop, and a job you stopped from here, only when the line is said to every Bot here. A stop on any other job stays; only words that name everything (「所有 Bot」「全都」) lift every stop you made. The line then wakes the Bot like any line, and the Bot goes on from it and answers you itself; its stopped work on the job in another conversation, which the line did not wake it to, opens again there on a note for that Bot alone, as before. A line filed under no job lifts only stops on the Bot as a whole. A stop that still holds (everything; a whole group or job stopped elsewhere) stays: the Bot can only read and answer the line, and is told the stop still holds, that it is lifted on its receipt, and not to say it is going on.

4. **只问进度的话不开新事 / A question about where the work stands opens no job.** 读句读成只问进度、归属读成新的一件事时，按「当场做完」算：Bot 为它查东西、跑命令都不开事。 / A line read as only asking where the work stands, which the filing reads as new work, counts as done in place: what the Bot looks up or runs for it opens no job.

5. **退回只按读句，直接退 / Send back only on a reading, at once.** 读句读出这句里有分句在对已交付、审查中或已通过的任务「现在这样」不满意，这句又归到那张任务（或它的分件；或只归到规划、而规划里交出的只有一张、做它的 Bot 还在），就直接转回返工：还在等你放行的那一版作废、卡片收起，放行过它的审查记一次漏看，已交付的事重新打开，做它的 Bot 带着你这句话被叫去改——你按停止停下的它在这件事上的活随之解除，和卡片上按退回一样（d6ab5d5）。原来那张「要转回返工吗？」的卡片不发了——那是应用在问你刚说过的话。你这句话下面写着「已转回返工：……」和一个撤销，读错了按撤销全部还原；同时叫醒的负责人被告知这件事已经转回返工、别另派人改。词表的读法、书记员记下的分件要求都不再算投诉。放行仍然只在卡片上按：打字永远不算放行。

   When the reader reads clauses of the line as objecting to work handed over, in review or approved as it stands now, and the line is filed under that ticket (or some of its parts; or under the plan as a whole, whose handed-over work is one ticket with its maker still here), the work goes back to rework at once: a hand-over still waiting on you is superseded and its card taken down, the reviews that let it through each count a miss, a delivered job is open again, and its maker is woken with your words — a Stop you pressed on its work in that job goes, as at 退回 on a card (d6ab5d5). The card asking 「要转回返工吗？」 is gone — it was the app asking about what you had just said. Under your line it says 「已转回返工：……」 with an undo that puts everything back if the reading was wrong; a lead woken by the same line is told the work went back and not to hand the fix to anyone else. Neither the word lists' reading nor a part-level entry the scribe made counts as a complaint any more. Approval stays a press on the card: no text ever approves.

## 取舍 / Trade-offs

- **问一句进度要花一次 Bot 的调用。** 原来应用照行回答不花钱；现在 Bot 自己答，加上读句那一次。读句跑在 Claude Agent 的 Claude 上时，用的是和那个账号上的 Bot 同一份额度；额度用完时读句失败，按第 2 条，停、继续都只当普通的话交给 Bot。 / A status question costs a Bot's call where the app's answer cost nothing, on top of the reading. With the reader on a Claude model of Claude Agent it spends the same plan as the Bots on that account; once that runs out readings fail, and by #2 a stop or a go on said in words is only a line for the Bot.
- **读句读错的退回真的退回了**，不再只多一张卡；撤销在你那句话下面，一按全部还原。 / A misread complaint really sends the work back, not just costs a card; the undo under your line puts it all back.
- **读句读不了时，「停」说了不算**，要按停止按钮。 / With no reading, saying 「停」 stops nothing; the Stop button does.
- **「算了」单说不再挂按钮**：它是给 Bot 的话。 / 「算了」 alone carries no buttons any more: it is a line for the Bot.
- **你在自己的私聊里说「继续」，Bot 在这里接着做、回你**，它停在别的会话里的这件事的活不再另开一段——Claude Code 跑的 Bot 在这里开的是新的会话，那边的上下文它要从记录里读回来。 / 「继续」 in your direct has the Bot go on there and answer you; its stopped work on that job in another conversation does not open beside it — a Claude Code Bot starts a fresh session here and reads back the other one's work from the records.

## 缺口 / Not done

- 「中断」「这一轮没写完」、重启通知和监督器的通知仍是应用写进对话的行（ADR 0058 管）。 / The 「中断」 and 「这一轮没写完」 lines, restart notices and the supervisor's notices are still the app's lines in the conversation (ADR 0058 governs them).
- 平常的一段不知道这个 Bot 身上还有哪些叫停：归不到哪件事的「继续」解除不了某件事上的叫停时，Bot 不会说是哪一条。 / An ordinary segment is not told which stops are on its Bot: when a go on filed under no job cannot lift a stop on one job, the Bot cannot say which it is.
- 你的话上的「继续」按钮（读成两样都有的话才挂）仍按旧规则解除这个 Bot 在各件事上的 Stop。 / The 继续 button on a line of yours (offered only when it read as both) still lifts that Bot's Stops on every job, by the old rule.
- 一句话退回几张任务时，撤销只还原最后一张。 / A line that sent back several tickets undoes only the last one.
