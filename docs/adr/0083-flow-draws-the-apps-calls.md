# 流程画出应用自己的调用 / The flow draws the app's own calls

Status: implemented 2026-10-11, at every engine level. Builds on ADR 0055 (reading), ADR 0057 (filing), ADR 0064 (built-in prompts), ADR 0077 (built-in models) and ADR 0082 (Roles); moves ADR 0082's page out of Settings.

## 背景 / Context

你说（2026-10-11）：「流程是不是就应该做成这个，现在的流程感觉体现不出来什么。」流程图画的是一轮里谁叫醒了谁：你的一句话，下面一棵 Bot 卡片的树，每张卡一句摘要。这基本是把对话换个形状再画一遍。你看不到的，是应用自己替你做的判断：这句话被读成了什么、归到哪件事、书记员记了哪条要求、哪个 Bot 判断不下场、看图判定为什么拦下、整理器改了什么。这些调用有的只在工作日志的 payload 里，有的（输入建议、判断下场的模型、每次调用照的提示词版本）哪里都没有，花费行也连不回是哪句话。

之前同一次讨论里定的方向：分工页（ADR 0082）是「应用会怎么处理一句话」的模板；绑定到一句话，就是「这次实际怎么处理的」；一件事里每句都这样连起来，就是流程。返工之后从看板倒推，要能落到具体是哪一次调用、照哪版提示词、跑在哪个模型上。你还定了两条：没归到任何一件事的话也要能打开它自己的流程（多半的「读错了」发生在没开工作的私聊里）；这次就把环节作为图里的节点整轮重排，而不是先在旁边加一条。分工也不该放在设置里：它是一张调试和定义的图，应该在对话进行中就能打开。

You said (2026-10-11): "Shouldn't the flow just be this? As it is, the flow doesn't really show anything." The flow drew who woke whom in a round: your line, a tree of Bot cards under it, a one-line summary on each. That is the conversation drawn again. What you could not see was what the app decided on your behalf: what a line was read as, which job it was filed under, what the scribe wrote down, which Bot judged not to join in, why the picture judge stopped a hand-in, what the organizer changed. Some of those calls lived only in work-log payloads; some (composer suggestions, a judgement's model, which prompt version any call ran with) nowhere, and spend rows could not be traced back to a line.

The direction set earlier in the same discussion: Roles (ADR 0082) is the template of how the app handles a line; bound to one line it is how it actually did; a job's lines joined up are the flow. Working back from rework, you should land on the very call, the prompt version it ran with and its model. You settled two more things: a line filed under no job opens its own flow too (most "it read me wrong" happens in a direct with no job open), and the steps go into the graph as nodes now, re-laying each round, rather than a strip beside it. Roles does not belong in Settings either: it is a map to debug and define with, to be opened while a conversation is going on.

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **每用一次内置调用，记一条 / One record per use of a built-in call.** 新表 `call_runs`：调用（`role`）、提示词和它的版本、提供方、模型和思考档，关于的话、轮次、事、任务、Bot、检查运行，结局（`ok`、`failed`、`unreadable`）和失败种类，每种调用一种形状的结论（`verdict`），原始回答（截 4000 字），花费行，用时。九个调用都写：读句（包括词表兜底；同一段 Bot 的话在另一轮里从缓存再读也记一条，标 `cached`，所以每个用到读法的轮次都看得到）、书记员、整理器（归档和整理两种，整理记它跟在哪一轮后面）、判断下场、输入建议、压缩上下文、看图判定（一次检查跑几批就几条，都带检查运行 id）、反思、完工复盘。在每个引擎级别都写。/ A new `call_runs` table: the call (`role`), its prompt and version, provider, model and thinking, the line, turn, job, ticket, Bot and check run it was about, how it came out (`ok`, `failed`, `unreadable`) and why not, a per-call verdict, the raw answer (clipped at 4000), the spend row and the time taken. All nine calls write one: reading (the word lists' fallback included; the same words of a Bot read again in another turn from the cache are a row of their own, `cached`, so every turn that applied a reading shows it), the scribe, the organizer (filing and settling; a settle notes the turn it came after), judgements, the composer, compaction, the picture judge (one row per batch, each with its check run), reflection and retrospective. Written at every engine level.

2. **环节在卡片旁边，不在卡片里 / Steps beside the cards, never among them.** `GET /v1/tasks/:id/trace` 多一个 `steps`：这件事的记录，和关于它的话、它的轮次的记录，不带原始回答（点开时 `GET /v1/call-runs/:id` 取全的）。整理器和规划事实把卡片读回给模型，读的还是原来的卡片，不会把应用自己的判断再喂回给它。/ `GET /v1/tasks/:id/trace` gains `steps`: the records on the job and about its lines and turns, without the raw answer (fetched whole from `GET /v1/call-runs/:id` when opened). The organizer and the plan facts read the cards back to a model and read the same cards as before; the app's own decisions are never fed back to it.

3. **环节是图里的节点 / Steps are nodes of the graph.** 每一轮里，环节和 Bot 的卡片一起交给 dagre 排：关于你这句话的（读句、归属、书记员、归档）按先后从你的卡往下排成一串，它叫醒的轮次挂在这一串的末尾；群里没点名的一句，每个 Bot 的判断各一步，接下的 Bot 的那一轮挂在它自己的判断下面；一轮里或为一轮做的（压缩、对它的话的读句、之后的整理）挂在那一轮下面，同一种调用合成一步，标次数；只关于这件事的（看图判定、反思、完工复盘）挂在它之前的最后一张卡下面。输入建议不画。每步写着结论（读成：停下；接着这件事；不下场：不是我的；没过 1/2），点开就地看模型、思考档、提示词版本、用时、原始回答，有「跳到这句」和「改这个调用」。轮头写环节数和异常数；没成或说「不」的用警示色，工具条可以一键点亮它们、暗掉其余；环节可以一键隐藏，按浏览器记住。/ Each round's steps and cards go to dagre together: what was asked about your line (reading, where it belongs, the scribe, filing) runs down from your card in order and the turns it woke hang from the end of that run; on a group line naming nobody each Bot's judgement is a step, and a Bot that took it up hangs from its own; what ran in or for a turn (compaction, readings of its words, the settle after it) hangs under that turn, one step per kind of call with a count; what is about the job only (the picture judge, reflection, retrospective) hangs under the last card before it. The composer is not drawn. Each step says what it concluded and opens in place with the model, thinking, prompt version, time, raw answer, "Jump to the line" and "Change this call". A round's header counts its steps and the ones that went wrong; those are drawn in the warning colour and can be lit with one press, the rest dimmed; the steps can be hidden with one press, remembered per browser.

4. **没归属的话有自己的流程 / A line in no job has its own flow.** `GET /v1/messages/:id/trace` 画一句话：它叫醒的轮次、那些轮次再叫醒的（最深 40 层、最多 300 轮），和关于它们的记录；Bot 的话代表它那一轮由之开始的你那句话；谁也没叫醒的一句就是它自己的卡加上它的环节。消息右键菜单：有归属的照旧给流程、看板、要点；没有的只给「流程」，开的是这一句的。标签页带着这一句，手机上的流程页也是；它跟着那条会话的轮次、消息和调用刷新。/ `GET /v1/messages/:id/trace` draws one line: the turns it woke and the ones those woke (40 deep, 300 turns at most), with the records about any of them; a Bot's line stands for the line of yours its turn started from; a line that woke nobody is its own card and its steps. A message's menu: one in a job keeps flow, board and plan; one in none has only "Flow", for that line. The tab carries the line, so does the phone's page, and it reloads on its conversation's turns, messages and calls.

5. **分工离开设置 / Roles leaves Settings.** 分工成了工具菜单里的一项（终端之后），宽窗口上是一个单例标签页（放在哪里照「窗口开在哪」，默认新标签页），新建窗格的菜单里也有；没有工作台的地方（手机、平板的远程连接）是代替列表的一页，有返回，先退出提示词编辑、再退出调用的页。环节上的「改这个调用」打开它并选中那个调用；卡片上的「在设置里看」说到某个调用的提示词时，也打开这里、在那段提示词的修改记录上；提示词页上那一行「去分工页」也是。设置里不再有「分工」：改过几个调用、哪个有冲突，看分工图上各个节点的标记。/ Roles is an item of Tools (after Terminal): a single tab on a wide window (placed as "Where windows open" says, a new tab by default), also in the new-pane menu; where there is no workbench (a phone, a tablet's remote connection) a page in place of the list, whose Back leaves the prompt editor, then the call's page, then the page. "Change this call" on a step opens it at that call; a card's "View in Settings" about one of a call's prompts opens it too, at that prompt's history, and so does the "Open Roles" line on Prompts. Settings has no Roles entry any more: which calls you changed and which has a conflict show as marks on the map's nodes.

## 后果 / Consequences

- **不回填 / No backfill.** 这之前的轮次没有调用记录，画出来只有卡片。/ Rounds from before this have no call records and show their cards only.
- **随会话删 / Gone with the conversation.** 删掉或清空会话时它的调用记录一起删（原始回答可能转述了你的话），像整理器的运行记录；每个会话留最近 2000 条。花费账本照旧保留。/ Deleting or clearing a conversation deletes its call records (a raw answer can paraphrase you), as the organizer's run log does; each conversation keeps its newest 2000. The spend ledger stays as it was.
- **Bot 读得到 / Bots can read them.** `call_runs` 在本机记录里有说明，Bot 能查；完工复盘可以引用具体的一次调用。/ `call_runs` is described in the records' catalog for Bots to query; a retrospective can cite a particular call.
- **一个旧 bug 没动 / An old bug left alone.** 判断下场没写成行时，花费行的 `judgement_id` 记的是一个不存在的 id（`participation.ts` 的 `bill(pending.id)`）。调用记录直接记花费行的 id，不靠它连。/ When a judgement row is not written, its spend row's `judgement_id` names an id with no row (`bill(pending.id)` in `participation.ts`). Call records keep the spend row's own id and do not join on it.
- **照样片的提示词 / The standard check's prompt.** 照样片的判定调用还没把提示词引用传给端点；它的调用记录写着提示词 id，不带版本。/ The standard check's judge does not yet pass its prompt reference to the endpoint; its records name the prompt id without a version.
- **取代 / Supersedes.** ADR 0082 第 2、4 条里「设置里的一页」和侧栏计数的位置，由第 5 条取代；分工图本身照旧。/ ADR 0082's decisions 2 and 4, as to the page being in Settings and where its count sits, are superseded by decision 5; the map itself stands.

## 2026-10-11 补记：环节在面板里打开 / A step opens in a panel

第 3 条里「点开就地看」改了：点开的一步不再挂在它的卡片下面（卡片一栏宽 208px，原始回答挤在里面，要在两个方向上滚才看得全），而是在流程右侧的面板里，宽 400px，高到工具条为止；手机宽度上是从底部拉起的面板，返回键先收起它。面板盖住了那一步的卡片时，画布滑开到卡片露出来。原始回答是 JSON 时画成可折叠的树。卡片的模型选择仍旧在卡片下面展开。

Decision 3's "opens in place" changed: an opened step no longer hangs under its card (a column 208px wide, where the raw answer had to be scrolled both ways to be read) but shows in a panel over the flow's right edge, 400px wide, down to the tools at most; as narrow as a phone it is a sheet from the bottom, which Back puts away first. When the panel covers the step's card, the board slides until the card is clear. A raw answer that is JSON is drawn as a tree you can fold. A card's model choice still unfolds under the card.
