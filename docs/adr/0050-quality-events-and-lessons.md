# 质量事件和教训代替事后复盘 / Quality events and lessons instead of after-the-fact reviews

Status: implemented at engine level 8 (`ENGINE_LEVELS.learning`), the learning half of ADR 0040's P5 (issue 28, first part). Opt-in like levels 5–7: no build raises a data folder past `ENGINE_LEVEL_BY_DEFAULT` (4) on its own.

[ADR 0040](0040-agent-kernel-the-job-owns-state.md) §7 要让「哪里出了问题」按事件的类型确定性地归类，「学到的东西」变成应用自己检查的规则，而不是事后问模型「是不是模型不行」、再把结论写进记忆。在这之前，一条链安静 15 分钟后开一次复盘调用（`route_review`），有把握的「模型的问题」再开一次学习调用（`route_learn`）写记忆或改技能；你在回复里说的话被当成对那次选路的反馈（`route_feedback`）。审计里，2 条「停工」被判成该换更强的模型；09-28 一个 Bot 把「全盘 grep 会超时」写进记忆，第二天又跑了一次同样的搜索（夹具 F-g）。

ADR 0040 §7 wants what went wrong filed by the event's own type, and what is learned turned into a rule the app checks itself — instead of asking a model afterwards whether the model was at fault and writing its answer into a memory. Before this, a chain quiet for 15 minutes got a review call (`route_review`), a confident "the model's fault" a learning call (`route_learn`) that wrote a memory or revised a skill, and what you said in reply counted as feedback on the model choice (`route_feedback`). In the audit two stalls were judged as needing a stronger model; on 09-28 a Bot wrote "a home-wide grep times out" into its memory and ran the same kind of search the next morning (fixture F-g).

## 决定 / Decisions

1. **版本边界 / Version boundary.** `ENGINE_LEVELS.learning = 8`；兼容地板仍是 6：7 级的二进制打开 8 级的库，只是不认那两张新表、又回去复盘链，什么也不会误读。两张表（`quality_events`、`lessons`）在开库时建，不随清空历史删除，只断开来源的轮次和消息。 / `ENGINE_LEVELS.learning = 8`; the floor stays 6: a level-7 binary on a level-8 database ignores the two new tables and goes back to reviewing chains, misreading nothing. The tables (`quality_events`, `lessons`) are made at open and survive clearing a history, which only drops the turn and message they came from.
2. **质量事件是工作日志的投影 / Quality events project the work log.** 8 级起，工作日志每写一条下列类型的事件，同一事务里就记一条质量事件，带上它说的是哪个 Bot、哪个模型（交付行上存着生产它的模型，清空历史后仍能归因）。类别只看事件类型： / From level 8, every work-log event of a kind below adds a quality event in the same transaction, with the Bot and model it is about (a hand-over keeps the model that made it, so this holds after a history is cleared). The category follows from the event's type alone:
   - 检查没过（交付时就没过，或你放行时门槛才没过；检查在交付前就在 → `execution`，交付后才定的 → `unclear`）、审查打回、你退回、你的投诉返工、搜索跑满超时、教训复犯 → `execution`（打回和投诉记在生产者头上）；
   - 交付之后你才第一次说的要求（你的原话、回答、批注或板上写的；导入的、采纳 Bot 建议的、书记员当投诉捕捉的不算；放给你确认的，确认时才算）→ `unclear`；
   - 回复复读、拒答、写到上限、超时、不完整 → `model`，不论这一轮在不在规划里；
   - 渲染失败或丢失、分件卡在能力天花板 → `pipeline`；
   - 被你推翻的放行 → `review_miss`（记在审查者头上）：投诉返工点名的审查者，以及你在卡片上退回的、审查者已经放行的交付；
   - 卡住、运行时出错、叫停后仍有副作用、监督器修复的停滞（规划停了、等待落空、一段结束却没交代）→ `orchestration`，只给维护者看，不进模型报表。端点连不上、忙或拒绝不算任何一类。

   撤回一次投诉，它记下的投诉和误放行一起撤掉。

   / Checks failed (at hand-over, or a gate failing only on your approval; a check there before the hand-over → `execution`, one defined after it → `unclear`), a review's reject, your send-back, your complaint's rework, a search killed by its timeout, a lesson recurring → `execution` (rejects and complaints are the producer's); a requirement first said after a hand-over, in your words (said, answered, annotated or written on the board; not imported, not a Bot's you accepted, not a complaint the scribe caught; one put to you counts once you confirm it) → `unclear`; a reply that loops, declines, hits its cap, runs over time or comes back incomplete → `model`, in a plan or not; a render that failed or got lost, a part stuck at the capability ceiling → `pipeline`; an approval you overturned → `review_miss` (the reviewer's): the reviewers a complaint names, and the reviewer who approved what you send back on its card; a stall, a crash, a stop broken, a stall the supervisor repairs (a stopped plan, a wait on nothing, a segment that ended without a word) → `orchestration`, for the maintainer only and outside the model report. An endpoint that could not be reached, was busy or refused is none of them. Taking a complaint back takes back the complaint and the misses it filed.
3. **你来标「模型问题」 / You mark the model's problems.** 流程图卡片的「模型选择」里多了「记为模型问题」：记一条 `model` 类的质量事件，再按一次撤回。它只进模型报表——不写记忆，也不改这个 Bot 跑在哪个模型上。卡片同时显示这一轮为什么跑在这个模型上（7 级的 `reason_code`）。 / A board card's model choice gains "Mark as the model's problem": one `model` quality event, taken back by pressing again. It feeds the model report only — no memory, no change to what the Bot runs on. The card also says why the turn ran on its model (level 7's `reason_code`).
4. **报表 / The report.** `GET /v1/quality/report?days=7`：按 Bot × 模型 × 规划类型给出交付数、通过数、被审查打回、被你退回、检查没过、投诉、失败形状、误放行，以及花费和每个通过的交付花了多少。它只是读数：要改，钉一个模型。 / Per Bot × model × plan kind: hand-overs, approvals, rejects by a review and by you, checks failed, complaints, failure shapes, review misses, the spend and the spend per approval. It only reads; to act on it, pin a model.
5. **失败签名教训 / Failure-signature lessons.** 只从会递归遍历目录的搜索（`grep -r`、`rg`、`find`、`du`、`ls -R`，浅的 `find -maxdepth` / `rg --max-depth` 不算）学：跑满超时被杀，就按它的种类（`commandSignature`：程序加 `-r`，`-rn`、`-R`、`--recursive` 算一类；再加上搜索的位置——家目录、`/`、工作区根、这一轮的任务目录用类名，更深的目录写出它本身；几个搜索根取范围最大的那个）记一条 `before_tool` 教训，归这个工作区（`scope` project，`scope_id` 工作区根），所有 Bot 共用。渲染、构建这类跑得久的命令不是错，不学。引号里的字面量、搜索词和带值参数的值（`-A 3`、`-g '*.ts'`）都不进签名；管道只看第一段，所以静态守卫解析不了的 `grep -r … ~ | head` 也有签名。教训先是**警告**：这一轮第一次再调这类命令时不跑，工具结果说上次怎样、怎么缩小范围；Bot 在同一轮里原样再调一次就放行。放行后又超时（复犯），教训改成**拦下**：这类命令从此不跑，哪个 Bot、哪一轮都一样。缩小到下一级目录就是另一类调用。命中、拦住、复犯次数记在教训上，每一轮只记一次。 / Only a search that walks a tree is learned from (`grep -r`, `rg`, `find`, `du`, `ls -R`; not a shallow `find -maxdepth` / `rg --max-depth`): killed by the timeout, it becomes a `before_tool` lesson on its kind (`commandSignature`: the program with `-r`, `-rn`, `-R`, `--recursive` alike; and the place it searched — home, `/`, the workspace root and the turn's work dir by class, anything deeper by the folder itself; of several roots the widest), held by this workspace (`scope` project, `scope_id` its root) and shared by every Bot. A render or a build running long is not a mistake and is not learned from. Quoted literals, patterns and the values of flags that take one (`-A 3`, `-g '*.ts'`) never enter it; a pipeline is read by its first stage, so the `grep -r … ~ | head` the static guard cannot parse has a signature too. A lesson first **warns**: the first call of that kind in a turn is not run, and the result says what happened and how to narrow it; repeating the call in the same turn runs it. Timing out again after that (a recurrence) turns it into a **block**: that kind of call is refused from then on, for every Bot and turn. A folder one level down is another kind of call. Hits, calls held back and recurrences are counted on the lesson, once per turn.
6. **你能看、能改、能停用 / Yours to read, change and retire.** 有教训时设置里多一个「教训」页：每条显示它管的那类调用、先警告还是拦下、命中次数，可以在警告和拦下之间切换、停用、恢复（`GET /v1/lessons`、`PATCH /v1/lessons/:id`，手机上也能用）。停用后又学到同一类，是一条新教训；旧的不能和它同时生效。 / With a lesson there, Settings gains a Lessons page: each shows the kind of call, whether it warns or blocks and how often it held one back, and can switch between warn and block, be retired or brought back (`GET /v1/lessons`, `PATCH /v1/lessons/:id`, from the phone too). Learned again while retired, it is a new lesson; the old one cannot be active beside it.
7. **事后复盘停掉 / After-the-fact reviews stop.** 8 级起链不再复盘、不再开学习调用，你的回复也不再记成选路反馈；`turn_route_decisions` 照记（默认模型的推断要读它）。旧代码留到 P6 删。 / From level 8 no chain is reviewed and no learning call is made, and your replies no longer count as feedback on the model choice; `turn_route_decisions` is still written (default models are inferred from it). The old code stays until P6 removes it.

## 缺口 / Not done

- **收窄的反思**（误放行、能力天花板 → 清单项或检查提议，你确认）：见 [ADR 0051](0051-narrowed-reflection.md)。**项目级技能**（`shared_skills`）、**报表的界面**：下一步。
- **记忆的契约不变**（ADR 0021/0035）；8 级起只是不再由学习调用写入。
- **只有搜索超时这一种签名**；工具 JSON 畸形、同一工具连败三次、跑得久的渲染还没有教训。

## 取舍 / Trade-offs

- **警告挡一次。** 一类命令超时过一次，下一轮的同类命令要多花一跳；换来的是 Bot 先看到「上次跑满 10 分钟」再决定，而不是又等 10 分钟。
- **签名对根目录用类名、对更深的目录用路径。** 任务目录在每件活里不同，用类名才能跨活生效；更深的目录用路径，缩小范围就能跑。
- **投影写在工作日志里。** 一个入口、同一事务；每写一条工作日志多读一次引擎级别。
