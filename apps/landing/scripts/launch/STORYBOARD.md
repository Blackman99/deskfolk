# Storyboard (as cut) — Deskfolk launch film, 30.5 s

120 BPM, bars on even seconds. Persistent actor: the teal request bubble — typed in shot 1, it lands in the group chat, becomes the plan's title on the flow board for the rest of the film, and finally turns into the Deskfolk mark (a teal speech bubble).

Signature transformations:
1. The request lifts out of the composer as a teal bubble and lands in the chat it was typed into; then it becomes the plan's title while tickets materialise beside it.
2. 开发's draft 「wc.py 写完了，测试通过。」 fills the frame on a hard cut, the app holds it against the commands that turn ran (0), underlines the claim and sends it back once; the terminal rises under it as it leaves.
3. The quiet clock (「上次变化 10 分钟前」) pops out of the board, turns into the app's 「叫回 写手」 and docks on the canvas where the new 写手 card appears.
